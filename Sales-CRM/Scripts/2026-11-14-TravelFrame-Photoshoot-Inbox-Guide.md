# TravelFrame Photoshoot Inbox Guide

Use this guide to draft replies about TravelFrame photoshoots on Instagram, Facebook, Threads, WhatsApp, and website chat.

**Answer the guest’s question about the photoshoot, suggest a sample set that fits, and ask for any missing details. The TravelFrame team confirms the shoot and sends a quote separately.**

## 1. Reply rules

- **Keep replies focused on TravelFrame photoshoots.** Cover the style, possible backdrops, how the shoot works, what the package includes, and sample sets cleared for sharing. Direct questions about tours, ebooks, or other services to the appropriate contact.
- **Keep pricing out of inbox replies.** Do not share hourly rates, package prices, starting prices, estimates, surcharges, discounts, promo codes, deposit amounts, or calculations. Leave out price lists, brochures with prices, links to pricing calculators, and pricing pages. The same rule applies if the guest asks again, sends a screenshot of an old price, or has already provided every detail.
- **Read the conversation before asking a question.** Use any date, group size, occasion, or sample set the guest has already mentioned. Ask for one missing detail per reply. If their first message asks about price and gives no trip details, you can ask for their dates and how many people will be in the photos in one sentence.
- **Summarize the details and hand them over to the TravelFrame team.** Only say the team is checking or that you’ve passed the details along once that has happened. Wait for the team to confirm availability, the photographer, the location, any hold on a time slot, or the booking itself.
- **Assistants draft replies.** Include only the conversation text in the guest-facing draft. Put handoff notes in the approved internal channel.

## 2. Write like TravelFrame

Write like a warm local friend in Hanoi who knows photography. Give the guest something they can picture, like a walk by the lake, a red outfit at Đồng Xuân Market, or shop windows lighting up on Tràng Tiền. If they feel awkward in front of the camera, tell them how the shoot can start.

| What to consider | How to write |
|---|---|
| Language and address | Use “we” and “you” in English. In Vietnamese, use “tụi mình” and “bạn,” or keep “anh/chị” if that’s how the conversation started. Use English if the guest writes in English or their preferred language is unclear. |
| Opening | Start with what the guest asked about. In a follow-up, go straight to the new point. |
| Length | Aim for 1–2 short sentences and under 200 characters when practical. If the guest asks to compare all three packages, use one line per package and end with one question. |
| Word choice | Use plain terms like “photo walk,” “natural photos,” and “street portraits.” Pick one detail about Hanoi that the guest can see in the sample set. |
| Ending | Give the guest one easy question or one clear next step. Use at most one emoji when it fits. Skip hashtags, long sign-offs, and pressure to book. |

> If you like the little moments between poses, a walk around the Old Quarter could suit you. Will it be just you or are you bringing someone along?

## 3. Photoshoot information

TravelFrame offers private photoshoots in Hanoi. Guests can choose portraits, candid photos as they walk, or a mix of both. The photographer can offer gentle posing guidance. Treat locations and times as suggestions until the team confirms them for the shoot.

### Match a sample set to the guest’s style

| What the guest likes | Sample set | What to mention |
|---|---|---|
| Candid couple photos in warm light | **Sunset Couple Stroll** | A late-afternoon walk with photos of the couple holding hands and sharing small moments along the way. |
| Daytime street-style portraits | **Hanna on Long Biên Bridge** | Daylight portraits framed by the bridge’s steelwork, with the city behind her. |
| Color and everyday street scenes in the Old Quarter | **Thin at Đồng Xuân Market** | A red outfit that stands out against the market stalls and shopfronts. |
| Night portraits | **Wuchr on Tràng Tiền** | Headlights and lit shop windows set the scene. |
| Áo dài, friends and family, incense village, or fashion editorial | **Samples for the requested style** | Note the style, group size, and preferred location. Pick an album cleared for sharing and check what’s possible for the specific shoot with the team. |

Pick one set that fits the request. If the guest is deciding between two styles, send two sets and explain the difference in one sentence. Check the images, caption, and linked page before sharing. If any of them shows prices or offers, share individual photos cleared for use in chat. Only say you’ve attached photos when they’re attached, and keep the photo credit when sharing images on their own.

### What each package includes

Use sample sets to show the look of a shoot. Use this table to explain the session length, coverage, and what guests receive with Basic, Premium, or VIP.

| Package | Minimum session length and coverage | What the guest receives |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic** | From 1 hour, 1 area | All original photos and 10–15 edited photos per hour of shooting. |
| **Premium** | From 2 hours, 2–3 areas | All original photos, 20 edited photos per hour of shooting, light makeup, a recap video up to 60 seconds, and 1 Polaroid print. |
| **VIP** | From 2 hours, 2–3 areas | All original photos, 30 edited photos per hour of shooting, light makeup, 1 outfit, a 2–3 minute video, and 5 Polaroid prints. |

- If the guest asks about delivery, the standard timing for these packages is original photos within 24 hours and edited photos and any included video within 48 hours. Check with the team if the guest needs them sooner. Use the term “original photos.”
- For groups of 3 or more, ask how many people will be in the photos and let the team confirm the setup. For makeup or outfits, ask who needs them and check with the team before confirming what’s included. Discuss transport or entry tickets only if the guest asks, and let the team confirm the details for the route.
- Suggest the Old Quarter, Hoàn Kiếm Lake, Tràng Tiền, or Long Biên Bridge according to the guest’s style. Avoid promising a specific spot, lighting conditions, or access to the residential railway corridor at Train Street.

## 4. What to ask before handing over

| What you need | How to ask | When you have enough |
|---|---|---|
| Shoot date or dates in Hanoi | “What dates will you be in Hanoi?” | A date or date range the team can check. If the guest only knows the month, mark it as tentative and share a sample set if it helps. |
| Number of people in the photos | “How many of you will be in the photos?” | A headcount. If the guest says “family” or “a group of friends,” ask how many people will be in the photos. |
| Photo style or occasion | “Do you like daytime street portraits or Hanoi after dark?” | The guest has described a preference, shared a reference photo, picked a set or package, or asked the team to suggest one. |
| Details that affect the shoot | Ask about video, outfits, location, session length, time of day, or a shorter walking route when the guest brings it up or it affects the plan. | The team has enough information to check the request. Flag any open details for confirmation. |

The core details are the date or date range, the number of people, and the style or package. If the guest is asking about a shoot soon or has a special request that needs checking, pass along what you know early. Guests can view sample sets without supplying a budget, phone number, or ID.

**Internal handoff notes** · Conversation ID · Date or date range and preferred time, if known · Number of people · Occasion and style · Package or sample set the guest chose · Requests to confirm · Missing details.

## 5. Sample replies by situation

Choose the reply that fits the current message. Update the details to match the conversation and remove any question the guest has already answered.

### The guest opens with a price question

> We do relaxed photo walks and street portraits in Hanoi. What dates will you be here, and how many of you will be in the photos?

### The guest asks about price again and has shared the date

> We’ll check what fits your shoot before we quote. How many of you will be in the photos on 12 December?

### The guest wants to see a sample set

> Here’s a couple’s shoot from a sunset walk, with warm light and little moments along the way. Do you like this look or prefer Hanoi after dark?

Use this reply when attaching **Sunset Couple Stroll**. If the guest has already sent a photo they like, point out a detail you can see and ask what they’d like to bring into their own photos.

### The guest is choosing a style

> The Old Quarter has busy lanes, and Tràng Tiền after dark has reflections in the shop windows. Which feels more like you?

### The guest is traveling solo and feels awkward posing

> We can start with a walk and give you a few easy prompts along the way. Would you feel more at home by the lake or in the Old Quarter?

### The guest is shooting with family or friends

> We can mix a few group portraits with photos of you walking together. How many of you will be in the photos?

### The guest asks which packages include video

> Premium comes with a recap video up to 60 seconds. VIP comes with a 2–3 minute video.

If the guest is still deciding, ask whether they’d prefer a short clip to share or a longer video to keep from the trip.

### The guest wants a quick comparison of all three packages

> Basic gives you a photo walk in one area and a set of edited photos.  
> Premium covers more than one area and adds a short recap video.  
> VIP adds an outfit and comes with a longer video.  
> Are you looking for photos, or would you like a video too?

### The guest asks when the photos will arrive

> We send the original photos within 24 hours. Edited photos and any video in your package follow within 48 hours.

### The guest wants to book and hasn’t shared a date

> Happy to check a time for your photo walk. What dates work for you in Hanoi?

### The guest has shared the details and asks what happens next

> Got it, a sunset shoot for two on 12 December. We’ll check the timing and shoot details with our team.

Use this reply when someone on the team is available to take over. If the guest asks about price at this point, you can say “We’ll confirm the shoot details and send you a written quote separately.”

### The guest asks about a location or inclusion that needs checking

> If you like railway backdrops, we can look at Long Biên Bridge. Would you prefer daylight or an evening shoot?

If the guest asks specifically about Train Street, explain that the team can’t promise access to the residential railway corridor. For áo dài, an incense village shoot, makeup for multiple people, or other requests that need checking, note the request and confirm it with the team before treating it as part of the package.

## 6. Before sending

- The first sentence answers what the guest asked, in the language of the conversation.
- Shoot details and package inclusions match the approved sources. Any attached photos are cleared for sharing, and the links and images are free of prices and offers.
- The reply contains no prices, estimates, or unconfirmed promises. Mention session length, photo counts, and delivery times only when relevant.
- The reply asks only for missing information. Once you have the essentials, summarize them and hand over to the team. Mark a quote as sent or a booking as confirmed only once that step is complete.
