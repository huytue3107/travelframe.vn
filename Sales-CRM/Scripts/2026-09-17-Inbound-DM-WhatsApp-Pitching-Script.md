## 1. Nguyên Tắc Trò Chuyện (Tone & Manner)

1. **"A Warm Local Friend":** Nhiệt tình, thân thiện, tôn trọng, không dùng văn phong robot hoặc ép bán (hard-selling).
2. **Value-First Before Pricing:** Luôn đồng cảm và giải quyết nỗi lo của khách trước (nỗi sợ tạo dáng đơ, sợ lừa đảo, đi một mình) trước khi đưa ra mức giá.
3. **Transparent & All-Inclusive:** Giới thiệu rõ mức giá trọn gói (Model A), khẳng định không có chi phí ẩn, đã bao gồm vé và cà phê/nước uống.
4. **Fast Turnaround Hook:** Luôn nhấn mạnh cam kết **24h Sneak Peek** để khách có ảnh đăng ngay trong kỳ nghỉ.

## 2. Kịch Bản 1: Khách Hỏi Giá Tổng Quát ("How much is your tour / photoshoot?")

> **Mục tiêu:** Định vị dịch vụ là Private Photo-Tour (không phải studio photoshoot thông thường), gửi Catalogue và gợi ý gói phổ biến nhất.

### Tin nhắn 1 (Phản hồi tức thì):
> *"Hi [Name]! Thanks so much for reaching out to TravelFrame Hanoi 🇻🇳!*
>
> *Unlike a standard photoshoot where you stand awkwardly in front of a backdrop, or crowded group tours rushing through shops, our experience is completely private: **a local friend who knows Hanoi's hidden corners guides you while capturing natural, candid moments and vertical video reels**.*
>
> *Here is a quick look at our 3 private all-inclusive packages:*
> - ⚡ **Quick Photo Walk (2 - 2.5h):** $75 USD (~1.95M VND) — Perfect for 1-2 guests wanting a relaxed stroll + 15 retouched photos.
> - ⭐ **Signature Story Tour (4 - 4.5h) [Most Popular]:** $160 USD (~4.15M VND) — 2 aesthetic moods, hidden cafes, street food, 35 retouched photos + 1 vertical Reel!
> - 🏛️ **Full-Day Cultural Odyssey (8 - 9h):** $290 USD (~7.5M VND) — Private car, pottery/incense village, 60 photos + short film.
>
> *I’ve attached our complete **2026 Visual Catalogue** for you here! When are you planning to visit Hanoi, and who are you traveling with?"*

## 5. Xử Lý Thắc Mắc & Hoài Nghi Phổ Biến (Objection Handling)

### Thắc mắc 1: "I'm awkward in front of the camera, what if I don't know how to pose?"
> *"Don't worry at all! 95% of our guests have never had professional photos taken. Our hosts never ask you to do stiff poses. We just walk, chat, have a coffee, and catch you looking natural and relaxed. We guide you gently on angles and lighting so you feel 100% comfortable."*

### Thắc mắc 2: "What happens if it rains heavily?"
> *"Hanoi weather can change, but you're in safe hands! If there’s heavy rain, we can reschedule free of charge. If timing doesn't allow, we switch to our covered heritage route: gorgeous French colonial covered arcades, historic art galleries, and cozy vintage cafes where rain creates a stunning cinematic mood!"*

### Thắc mắc 3: "Are there any extra fees during the tour?"
> *"None at all! Our pricing is 100% all-inclusive. Your dedicated host, photographer, editing, entrance fees, and specialty beverage stops are all covered in the package price."*
