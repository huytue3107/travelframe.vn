Land in Hanoi with $0 on your phone and one queue away from a bad start. 🛬

The trap: the SIM kiosk at Noi Bai baggage claim. $15–25 for a tourist SIM with capped data — and your passport handed over the counter.

The move: buy an eSIM before you fly. Here is why it wins:

💰 Around $6–8 (150k–200k VND) — roughly half the airport price, and data that doesn't run out on day two
⚡ Connects the second the wheels touch down. No queue. No passport handover.
📱 One QR code scanned at home. Setup takes 2 minutes, do it before your flight.
🏦 Your home SIM stays on for bank OTPs — the eSIM quietly runs data for Grab and Google Maps.

One thing we tell every friend landing here: set it up at home, not at the airport. Airport wifi dies exactly when you need it most.
