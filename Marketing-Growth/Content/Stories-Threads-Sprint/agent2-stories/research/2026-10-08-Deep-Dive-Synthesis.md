## Executive read

The strongest owned-channel evidence favors concise first-timer utility over service promotion: practical Reels, money checks, language snippets, packing, and carefully sourced safety guidance. Reach, saves, and follows are measurable; attributable Story outcomes and post-to-DM-to-booking results are not. So use performance data to choose topics and structures, never as proof that a specific hook caused the result. For any sensitive/travel facts, the canonical verified-facts register outranks draft copy, research summaries, and competitor material.
