# TravelFrame — five Hanoi street-moment Reel drafts

**Lead revision:** Rebuilt around the café Reel's ordinary-place-and-time → unexpected real-life scene structure. These are not photography tutorials.

**Production and truth gate:** Every location/time below is a proposed scouting window, not a claim that the event happens there regularly or will happen on schedule. Open on the actual surprise, filmed by TravelFrame at the stated place and time. Use a concept only if that exact scene is naturally present in the new footage; never stage or imply an event that was not recorded. If it does not happen, skip or reschedule the concept. Obtain venue permission where required and consent from every identifiable person before filming/using them; exclude or blur everyone else, including children. Do not publish footage if consent is unavailable. Do not reuse another creator's footage or audio. Mute source audio and add no borrowed music, dialogue, or sound effects.

**Format:** 9:16, about 8–12 seconds, visual event visible in the first frame, one short overlay line only, no spoken setup. Use only newly captured footage unique to that Reel. The shot list is a capture plan, not a record of an event already observed.
