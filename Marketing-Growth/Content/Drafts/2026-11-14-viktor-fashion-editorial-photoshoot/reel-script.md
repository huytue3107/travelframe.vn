# One dress, three Old Quarter backdrops

## Hook (0-3s)

One red dress. Three backdrops. All in Hanoi's Old Quarter.

## Beats

1. The market front. Đồng Xuân Market's sign, parked bikes and the traffic do half the styling.

2. A closed metal shutter. Plain, grey and free. A bright dress pops against it.

3. Parked motorbikes. Always ask the owner or the attendant first. Pro move: a smile and a thank-you before you sit, not after.

4. Then change once. A second look, like streetwear on a cyclo, turns one walk into two shoots.
