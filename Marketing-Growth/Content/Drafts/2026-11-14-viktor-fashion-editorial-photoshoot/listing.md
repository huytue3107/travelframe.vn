# Fashion Editorial Photoshoot in Hanoi

## Listing title

Hanoi: Fashion Editorial Photoshoot in the Old Quarter

Short title (under 60 characters): Hanoi Fashion Editorial Photoshoot, Old Quarter

## Summary

Turn Hanoi's markets, metal shutters, motorbikes and cyclos into your backdrop. A styled street shoot in the Old Quarter with a local photographer who plans the looks, the locations and the light with you.

## Highlights

- Editorial-style portraits around Đồng Xuân Market and Old Quarter streets
- Real street backdrops: market signs, shutters, parked bikes, cyclos
- Looks and references planned with you before the shoot
- Strong posing direction for a lookbook or profile refresh
- All original photos within 24 hours, edited photos within 48 hours

## Full description

Some people want soft, candid travel photos. This one is for people who want a look.

Before the shoot, send us two or three reference photos and tell us what you plan to wear. We match each outfit to a backdrop: a bright dress against the grey of a closed shutter, streetwear on a cyclo, sunglasses and the market sign behind you. On the day we direct more than on a regular photo walk, so you get strong poses as well as in-between moments.

The street stays a street. We ask shop owners, attendants and drivers before using their shopfronts, bikes or cyclos.

## What you'll do

**Look 1, the market.** Đồng Xuân Market's front, stacked goods and parked motorbikes.

**Look 2, the street.** Metal shutters, yellow walls and a cyclo, if the driver agrees.

**Optional third area.** A lamp-lit café front or the lake, on Premium and VIP (2 to 3 areas).

Meeting point: agreed with you after booking, usually in the Old Quarter. [VERIFY]

## Who it's for

- Fashion lovers who pack outfits with photos in mind
- Creators refreshing a profile or building a lookbook
- Anyone who wants Hanoi photos that look styled, not snapshot

## Pricing

Priced per hour for up to 2 guests. We suggest Premium or VIP so you have time for 2 or more looks.

| Tier | Price | Minimum | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | 30 USD per hour | 1 hour | 1 area, all original photos, 10 to 15 edited photos per hour |
| Premium | 60 USD per hour | 2 hours | Basic plus 2 to 3 areas, light makeup, a short recap video up to 60 seconds, 1 printed polaroid, 20 edited photos per hour |
| VIP | 99 USD per hour | 2 hours | Premium plus an outfit, a 2 to 3 minute video, 5 printed polaroids, 30 edited photos per hour |

Two looks with a short video: Premium for 2 hours is 120 USD. With an outfit and a longer video: VIP for 2 hours is 198 USD.

Personal use only. Shoots for a brand or a commercial campaign are quoted separately. [VERIFY]

## Included

- Private photographer-guide for the hours booked
- Look and location planning before the shoot
- All original photos within 24 hours
- Edited photos (and video on Premium and VIP) within 48 hours

## Not included

- Full glam makeup, hair styling or a stylist (Premium and VIP include light makeup) [VERIFY]
- Taxi or Grab between areas
- Food, drinks and any tips for cyclo drivers or shop owners
- Train Street. We don't include the residential railway corridor in any route.

## Good to know

- Bring your outfits in a bag and wear the first one. We find a café to change between looks. [VERIFY]
- Comfortable shoes for walking, heels for the shots.
- In rain we move to covered spots or reschedule. [VERIFY]
- Cancellation policy. [VERIFY]

## FAQ

**I'm not a model. Can I still book this?**
Yes. We direct every pose, and we show you the shots as we go.

**How many outfits can I bring?**
Two looks fit well in 2 hours. Want more? Tell us and we plan the time. [VERIFY]

**Can we shoot inside the market?**
We shoot outside and around the market. Inside, only where stall owners say yes.

**When do I get the photos?**
All original photos within 24 hours. Edited photos and video within 48 hours.
