Hanoi is a pork-heavy food city. That is the trap.
The move: stay close to the Old Quarter.
🕌 The city's only mosque sits on Hàng Lược Street.
🍜 Halal Vietnamese food is right beside it (go before the afternoon).
🔥 At night: Egyptian grills and halal Indian curry, a short walk away.
💬 Always ask: no pork, no lard, no alcohol in the food?
