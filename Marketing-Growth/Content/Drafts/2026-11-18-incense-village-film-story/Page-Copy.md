# Incense Village Photoshoot: Harry's Film Portraits at Quảng Phú Cầu

"Red on red on red." That is how Harry captioned one of his sets from Quảng Phú Cầu (Quang Phu Cau). It is the incense village about 35 km south of Hanoi.

Harry is our photographer. He photographs portraits in the village, some of them on film. This is the story of that place through his frames, and how to plan a photoshoot of your own.

## Quick answer

- **Where:** Quảng Phú Cầu, a craft village about 35 km south of central Hanoi.
- **When:** dry, sunny days, when families fan the bundles out to dry. Rain keeps them inside.
- **What to wear:** white, black, soft green or deep blue. The yard already has plenty of red.
- **Rule one:** this is a workplace. Ask before you step into a yard, and never step on the bundles.
- **Directions, fees and etiquette:** see our [Quảng Phú Cầu visit guide](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/cam-phu-cau-incense-village-photography-guide).

## A working village, painted red

Families here have made incense for more than a century. Bamboo is split into thin sticks, dyed red or lotus pink, and dried in the sun, as [VietnamPlus](https://www.vietnamplus.vn/lang-huong-quang-phu-cau-giu-nhip-nghe-tram-nam-vuon-minh-thanh-diem-hen-du-lich-van-hoa-post1147316.vnp) described on 10 November 2026.

Machines now do the heavy splitting. Mixing, dyeing and fanning out the bundles are still done by hand. On a sunny day, whole yards turn red.

## Quảng Phú Cầu photography: three frames from Harry's September sets

In early September 2026, Harry posted three Quảng Phú Cầu sets on Instagram. Here is one frame from each, and why it works.

The photos on this page are stock photos from Pexels, credited under each one. Harry's own frames stay on his Instagram, so each set links to his post.

### 1. White against the red

A woman in a long white dress and a conical hat walks away from the camera, arms open, between rows of red bundles. Harry's caption: "A frame full of red, a soul full of wonder."

**Why it works:** white is the one color the yard does not have. The hat gives the pose a clear shape. [See the set on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dc29m37Dx0y/) (4 September 2026).

### 2. Black, from above

A woman in a black off-the-shoulder dress holds her conical hat and looks up at the camera. The angle is high, so the bundles turn into a pattern. Harry tagged the set #Filmlook.

**Why it works:** black stays crisp against red, and height turns rows of bundles into texture. Villagers have built iron stairs and viewing platforms for visitors who want that angle. [See the set on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dc3FErND_Yf/) (4 September 2026).

### 3. One figure in a sea of crimson

A person in a white shirt and a conical hat sits among the bundles, small in a frame that is almost all red. Harry's caption: "Lost in a sea of crimson incense."

**Why it works:** one small figure gives the red a sense of scale. [See the set on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dc_Crtfo_Rv/) (7 September 2026).

## Why film suits this village

Film slows you down. There is no screen to check, and a roll holds a limited number of frames. You wait for the moment instead of shooting a hundred.

That pace fits Quảng Phú Cầu, where bundles are fanned out by hand and dried by the sun, one yard at a time. It also asks something of the person in front of the camera. Give yourself a few minutes to settle into a yard before the first frame.

This is our view, not a package. If you are curious about film, say so when you message us. We will tell you what we can plan for your dates.

## How an incense village photoshoot works

If you want photos of yourself in the village, we can plan the trip with you. Your photographer-guide talks with the families before you step into their yards. We then photograph you in two or three of them.

- **Pick a dry day.** Keep a backup day in case of rain.
- **Mid-morning is a safe bet.** By then most yards have their bundles out. Midday sun is harsh on faces, so look for shade at the edge of a yard.
- **Bring small notes.** Some yards and viewing platforms ask for a small fee.

Directions, fees and etiquette are in the [visit guide](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/cam-phu-cau-incense-village-photography-guide). Packages for a Hanoi shoot are on our [Hanoi photoshoot](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/hanoi-photoshoot) page.

## What to wear

| Color | Against the red |
|---|---|
| White | The one color the yard does not have |
| Black | Crisp edges and strong contrast |
| Soft green or deep blue | Calm against the red |
| Red or pink | Disappears into the background |

An áo dài and a conical hat is the classic look here. See [four áo dài looks Harry shot in Hanoi](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/ao-dai-photoshoot-hanoi).

**Pro move:** give your hands a job. A conical hat works well, and it stops the stiff-arms look.

## Be a good guest

Ask before you enter a yard, and before you photograph someone up close. Do not step on, move or sit on the bundles unless the family invites you. People carry, stack and drive carts here, so keep the paths clear.
