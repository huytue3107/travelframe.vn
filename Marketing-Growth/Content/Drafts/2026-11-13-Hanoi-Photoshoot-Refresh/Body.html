Where we shoot

Every route is planned around the light, your pace and current local conditions. Popular backdrops include:

  • Old Quarter lanes: weathered shopfronts, craft streets, tube houses and hidden cafés.
  • Hoàn Kiếm Lake and the Huc Bridge: best in soft morning light.
  • French Quarter and the Opera House: colonial facades and wide boulevards.
  • Long Biên Bridge and the Phùng Hưng railway arches: Hanoi's railway heritage, photographed from public areas.
  • St. Joseph's Cathedral, Temple of Literature, West Lake: on longer walks.

Note on Train Street: we do not include or guarantee access to the residential railway corridor. Access changes and authorities enforce the railway safety zone. Read our Train Street safety guide.

What to wear

Solid, contrasting colors photograph best against Hanoi's yellow walls and green shutters. Bring comfortable shoes, sunglasses or a vintage accessory, and a relaxed mindset. Cover shoulders and knees if we visit temples.

Frequently asked questions

I'm awkward in front of the camera. Will this work for me?

Yes. We photograph you while you explore, so most shots are natural moments rather than poses. We give light direction only when you want it.

How long should my photoshoot be?

One hour covers one area, which is what Basic includes. Premium and VIP start at 2 hours and cover 2–3 areas, for example the Old Quarter plus the lake or the French Quarter.

When will I get my photos?

All original photos within 24 hours. Edited photos and video within 48 hours.

What time of day is best?

Early morning (soft light, fewer crowds) or late-afternoon golden hour. We suggest a time once we know your dates and the forecast.

Can you take us to Train Street?

We don't include or guarantee the residential railway corridor. We can photograph Hanoi's railway heritage from public areas such as the Phùng Hưng arches or Long Biên Bridge.

What if it rains?

We adjust the route toward covered streets, cafés and arcades, or reschedule. The rain policy is confirmed in writing when you book.
