**Slide 2:**
> 🍜 **"Ngon quá!"** (Ngon kwah)
> = "Delicious!"
> Say this after eating. Watch the cook's face light up.

**Slide 3:**
> ☕ **"Cà phê sữa đá"** (Kah feh sua dah)
> = Vietnamese iced milk coffee
> The magic words at any café.

**Slide 4:**
> 😂 **"Ơi!"** (Uhh-ee)
> = Hey! / Excuse me!
> The universal Vietnamese attention-getter.

**Slide 5:**
> 💰 **"Bao nhiêu?"** (Bow nyew)
> = How much?
> Essential at every market stall.

**Slide 6:**
> 📸 **"Chụp ảnh được không?"** (Choop anh dook kohng)
> = Can I take a photo?
> Always ask. Always smile. Always get a better photo.
