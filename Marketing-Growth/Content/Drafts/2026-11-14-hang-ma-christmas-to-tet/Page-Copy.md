# Hàng Mã After Christmas: When Hanoi's Tinsel Street Turns Red and Gold for Tết

**Quick answer:** Hàng Mã doesn't go quiet after Christmas. Within days, the tinsel and Santa suits make way for red lanterns, gold paper and lucky-money envelopes for Tết, the Lunar New Year. In 2027, Tết falls on Saturday, February 6, so the red-and-gold weeks run from late December to early February. [VERIFY: ngày đổi hàng thực tế năm nay — team quan sát cuối tháng 12]

## Why Hàng Mã changes every few weeks

Hàng Mã is Hanoi's festival-supply street. Shops sell whatever the next celebration needs: lanterns for Mid-Autumn, masks for Halloween, tinsel in December. After Christmas, the next big date is Tết, and the street follows it.

## Christmas vs. Tết on Hàng Mã

| | December | January to early February |
|---|---|---|
| Main colours | Silver, green, white | Red and gold |
| What's on sale | Trees, tinsel, Santa hats, snowmen | Lanterns, lucky-money envelopes, paper couplets, kumquat and peach-blossom decorations |
| Crowd | Busiest on the evenings before December 24 | Gets busier in the last two weeks before Tết |
| Best for photos | Shopfronts lit after dark | Red lanterns in late-afternoon light |

[VERIFY: danh mục hàng Tết bán trên chính phố Hàng Mã năm nay — kiểm tra thực địa]

## When to go

- **Late December:** both seasons can overlap for a few days. Some shops still show Christmas stock while others start on red. [VERIFY]
- **January:** red and gold take over. Weekday afternoons are calmer.
- **The last two weeks before Tết (late January 2027):** the busiest time, as Hanoi families shop for the holiday. Expect motorbikes stopping to load up.
- **Tết week itself:** many shops close for a few days around February 6. Check our [Tết 2027 in Hanoi guide](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/tet-in-hanoi) for what stays open.

## Photo tips

- Red lanterns look best in soft light. Try 3:30–5pm, before the shop lights come on.
- Stand on the shop side of the pavement, out of the way of motorbikes.
- Some shops ask for a small fee if you photograph inside. Buying something small first is the polite way in.
- Wear one plain colour. White, cream or navy stands out against a wall of red.

For the full list of corners and timing, see our [Hàng Mã Christmas photo guide](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/hang-ma-street-christmas-photo-spots). Most of the spots work in both seasons.

## Practical notes

- Hàng Mã is in the Old Quarter, a short walk north of Hoàn Kiếm Lake.
- Hanoi is cool in January, with cold, damp evenings. A light jacket helps. See our [Hanoi weather in December guide](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/hanoi-weather-december) for what to pack.
- Lucky-money envelopes and small paper decorations make easy, light souvenirs. [VERIFY: giá tham khảo nếu muốn nêu]
