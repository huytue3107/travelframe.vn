# 3 mistakes that cost first-timers their first day in Hanoi

## Hook (0-3s)

Landing in Hanoi soon? Three mistakes that waste your first day. Number three costs money.

## Beats

1. Mistake one: changing all your cash at the airport. Change a small amount for the ride, then compare rates in town. [VERIFY]

2. Mistake two: photographing people without asking. A smile and a pointed camera usually gets a yes. Some vendors may ask for a small fee, so agree first.

3. Mistake three: ordering where no price is shown. Point at the menu or ask the price before you sit. Pro move: learn bao nhiêu, which means how much.
