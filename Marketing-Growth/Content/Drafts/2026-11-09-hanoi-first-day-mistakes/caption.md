Landing in Hanoi soon? Three mistakes that waste your first day. Number three costs money. 🇻🇳

1. Changing all your cash at the airport. Change a little for the ride, then compare rates in town. [VERIFY]
2. Photographing people without asking. Smile, point at the camera, wait for a yes.
3. Ordering where no price is shown. Ask first, or point at the menu.

Pro move: learn bao nhiêu (how much). It solves half your problems.
