# Friends and Family Photo Walk in Hanoi

## Listing title

Hanoi: Private Photo Walk for Friends and Families

Short title (under 60 characters): Hanoi Group Photo Walk for Friends and Families

## Summary

A relaxed walk around Hoàn Kiếm Lake and the Old Quarter for your group, with a local photographer who gets everyone in the frame, including the person who usually takes the photos.

## Highlights

- Everyone in the photos, including whoever usually holds the phone
- Group shots, pairs and solo portraits in one walk
- An easy pace with café stops, planned around kids and older travelers
- A local photographer-guide who knows the quieter corners
- All original photos within 24 hours, edited photos within 48 hours

## Full description

Group trips end with the same camera roll: lots of views, a few group shots, and none of the person who took them.

On this walk your photographer does the shooting while you all enjoy the city. We start with the whole group while energy is high. Then we split into pairs (sisters, best friends, parents and kids) and finish with a few solo portraits for anyone who wants them.

We plan the route around your group. Shorter walks and a café break for little ones and grandparents. More stops and after-dark streets for a group of friends.

## What you'll do

**Lakeside.** Hoàn Kiếm Lake paths and benches, with the Turtle Tower behind you.

**Old Quarter.** Café windows, yellow walls and lantern-lit streets.

**After dark (optional).** St. Joseph's Cathedral and the lake with the lights on.

Meeting point: agreed with you after booking, usually near Hoàn Kiếm Lake. [VERIFY]

## Who it's for

- Families traveling together, from kids to grandparents
- Groups of friends, bachelorette and birthday trips
- Parents and adult children on a trip together

## Pricing

Priced per hour. The tier price covers 2 guests. From the 3rd guest it is 15 USD per person per hour.

| Tier | Price | Minimum | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | 30 USD per hour | 1 hour | 1 area, all original photos, 10 to 15 edited photos per hour |
| Premium | 60 USD per hour | 2 hours | Basic plus 2 to 3 areas, light makeup, a short recap video up to 60 seconds, 1 printed polaroid, 20 edited photos per hour |
| VIP | 99 USD per hour | 2 hours | Premium plus an outfit, a 2 to 3 minute video, 5 printed polaroids, 30 edited photos per hour |

Examples:
- 3 guests, Basic, 1 hour: 30 + 15 = 45 USD
- 4 guests, Premium, 2 hours: 120 + 60 = 180 USD

## Included

- Private photographer-guide for the hours booked
- Group, pair and solo shots in one session
- All original photos within 24 hours
- Edited photos (and video on Premium and VIP) within 48 hours

## Not included

- Taxi or Grab between areas
- Entrance tickets, food and drinks
- Train Street. We don't include the residential railway corridor in any route.

## FAQ

**Our kids won't sit still. Is that okay?**
Yes. Moving kids make the best candid photos. We keep the posed group shot short.

**Can we split into smaller groups?**
Yes. We plan time for pairs and solo portraits after the group shots.

**How is the price worked out?**
Each tier covers 2 guests. From the 3rd guest there is a per-person hourly add-on, included in the price you see when you choose the number of travelers.

**When do we get the photos?**
All original photos within 24 hours. Edited photos and video within 48 hours.
