# Someone is always missing from the group photo

## Slide 1 — Someone is always missing

Usually the one holding the phone. Here is how to fix it on your Hanoi trip.

## Slide 2 — The trap

Ten photos of the view. Three of the group. None of whoever took them.

## Slide 3 — The move

Hand the camera to someone who walks with you. Then everyone is in every shot.

## Slide 4 — Big group first

Start with everyone while energy is high. Then pairs, then solo portraits.

## Slide 5 — Give the shy one a role

Lead the walk, carry the flowers, order the coffee. They forget the camera.

## Slide 6 — Let the slowest walker set the pace

Short walks, café stops, shade in between. Pro move: tell us ages when you book.

## Slide 7 — Tag your group photographer

Tag the one who always takes the photos. DM PHOTO with your group size and dates.
