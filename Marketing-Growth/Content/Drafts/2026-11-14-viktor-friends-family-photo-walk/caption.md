Someone is always missing from the group photo. Usually the one holding the phone. 🤳

On a group trip you get ten photos of the view, three of the group and none of whoever took them.

The fix: hand the camera to someone who walks with you. Start with everyone while energy is high, then pairs, then solo portraits. Give the shy one a job and let the slowest walker set the pace.

Pro move: tell your photographer everyone's ages before the walk, so the route fits the group.
