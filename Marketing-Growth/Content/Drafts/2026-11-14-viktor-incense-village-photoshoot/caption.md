All that red? Incense sticks, drying in the sun. About an hour from Hanoi. ❤️

Quảng Phú Cầu is a craft village about 35 km south of the city. Families make incense by hand and fan the bundles out across their yards to dry.

It is a workplace, so ask before you step into a yard and walk around the bundles. It only works on dry days, so keep a backup day if you can.

Pro move: learn cảm ơn (thank you) and say it on the way out.
