# The red village an hour from Hanoi

## Hook (0-3s)

All that red? Incense sticks, drying in the sun. About an hour from Hanoi.

## Beats

1. This is Quảng Phú Cầu, a craft village about 35 km south of the city. Families make incense by hand and fan the bundles out to dry.

2. It is a workplace, not a photo set. Ask the family before you step into a yard, and walk around the bundles. Pro move: learn cảm ơn, thank you, and say it on the way out.

3. It only works on dry days. When it rains, the bundles stay inside. Keep a backup day if you can.

4. Wear white, black or soft green. Against all that red, you will stand out.
