# DM follow-up - PHOTO

Here is your solo Hanoi pose guide:

1. Walk slowly past the camera and look slightly to the side.
2. Adjust your sleeve, bag strap, glasses or hair.
3. Pause at a doorway, shift your weight and look beyond the camera.
4. Interact with the place: hold a coffee, browse a menu or watch the street.
5. Repeat one small movement for three frames instead of freezing in one pose.

You do not need to memorize these. A good shoot should guide you one prompt at a
time.
