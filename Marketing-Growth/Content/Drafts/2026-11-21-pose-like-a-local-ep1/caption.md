Think you are not photogenic? Three fixes we use with every guest. 📸

✗ Weight on both feet → ✓ shift to the back leg
✗ Hands with nowhere to go → ✓ give them a job: coffee, bag strap, hat
✗ Staring at the lens → ✓ look at the street, glance back on three
