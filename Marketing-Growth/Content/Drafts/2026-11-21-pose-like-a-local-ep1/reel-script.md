# Pose Like a Local, ep.1: 3 fixes for I'm not photogenic

## Hook (0-3s)

Think you are not photogenic? Three fixes we use with every guest.

## Beats

1. Problem: weight on both feet, standing like a soldier. Fix: shift your weight to the back leg.

2. Problem: hands hanging with nowhere to go. Fix: give them a job. A coffee cup, a bag strap, your hat.

3. Problem: staring at the lens. Fix: look at the street, then glance back on three.
