# Keyword research for TravelFrame (Hanoi photo tours)

Date: 2026-09-15  
Domain: travelframe.vn  
Target market: English-speaking international travelers (US, UK, Australia, Europe, Singapore)  
Intent focus: Commercial booking intent and high-intent travel planning  

## Opportunity summary

Most search volume for Hanoi photography clusters around two poles:
1. High-volume general photo spots (people wanting free spots for their own phones).
2. Lower-volume, high-intent commercial terms (people wanting to hire a photographer or book a private photo walk).

TravelFrame should focus its website pages on the commercial terms first. A small site with 8 monthly visitors cannot rank on day one for "vietnam travel", but it can rank on page one for "private photographer in hanoi" and "solo traveler photo tour hanoi" within 60 to 90 days.
