# 7 Best Day Trips from Hanoi (2026): Where to Go and How to Get There

{{IMG:13878582|A traveler looks over the karst valley and river of Tràng An, Ninh Bình}}

*By TravelFrame Editorial Team · Updated November 2026*

Hanoi is a good base for short escapes. Within two hours you can reach limestone valleys, a famous bay, and villages that still make pottery and incense by hand.

Here are seven day trips, from the closest to the longest, with honest notes on travel time.

## At a glance

| Day trip | Distance from Hanoi | Travel time each way | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bát Tràng ceramic village | about 13 km | about 30 min | Half day, hands-on pottery |
| Quảng Phú Cầu incense village | about 35 km | about 1 hour | Photography |
| Đường Lâm ancient village | about 45 km | 1–1.5 hours | Slow village life |
| Perfume Pagoda | about 60 km | 1.5–2 hours, plus a boat | Pilgrimage, scenery |
| Ninh Bình (Tràng An, Tam Cốc) | about 100 km | 1.5–2.5 hours | Karst landscapes, boat rides |
| Hạ Long Bay | about 165 km | 2.5–3 hours | Bay cruise (overnight is better) |
| Santorini Park and Yên Sở Park | in the city | 30–40 min | A light half day |

*Times are by car and depend on traffic. Distances are approximate.*

## 1. Ninh Bình: Tràng An and Tam Cốc

The most popular day trip from Hanoi. Rowing boats glide between limestone peaks, through caves and past rice fields.

- **Getting there:** private car, tour bus or train. The drive is about 1.5–2.5 hours; the train takes a little over 2 hours.
- **Tip:** leave early. A full day is tight but doable.

{{IMG:34439750|Rowing boats with visitors on a green river below limestone hills in Hoa Lư, Ninh Bình}}

## 2. Hạ Long Bay

Thousands of limestone islands rise from the sea. It is a long day: about 2.5–3 hours each way on the expressway.

- **Honest advice:** a day trip works, but an overnight cruise lets you see the bay at sunset and sunrise without rushing.

{{IMG:38116420|Cruise boats among limestone islands in Hạ Long Bay}}

## 3. Bát Tràng ceramic village

The closest trip on this list, about 13 km from central Hanoi. Watch potters at work, try the wheel yourself, and browse shops full of glazed ceramics.

- Read our [Bát Tràng pottery museum guide](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/bat-trang-pottery-museum-photography-guide).

{{IMG:36713995|A craftsperson polishes a large decorative vase in a Vietnamese pottery workshop}}

## 4. Quảng Phú Cầu incense village

In Ứng Hòa district, about 35 km south of Hanoi. On dry days, bundles of red incense sticks are fanned out to dry in yards and lanes. It is one of the most photogenic scenes near the city.

- Read our [Quảng Phú Cầu incense village guide](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/cam-phu-cau-incense-village-photography-guide).
- **Be polite:** this is a working village. Ask before photographing people, and stay off the drying racks.

{{IMG:23715909|Red incense sticks drying outdoors while a worker in a conical hat tends them}}

## 5. Đường Lâm ancient village

About 45 km west of Hanoi, near Sơn Tây. Old laterite houses, ancestral halls and quiet lanes. There is a small entrance fee for adults, about 20,000 VND. Check on arrival.

## 6. Perfume Pagoda (Chùa Hương)

About 60 km southwest of Hanoi. You reach the main temple complex by rowing boat along the Yến River, then on foot or by cable car.

- **Timing:** the pilgrimage season after Lunar New Year is very crowded. Outside the festival months, the trip is much calmer.

## 7. Santorini Park and Yên Sở Park

Not a true day trip, but a good half day if you want a change of scene without leaving the city. Read our [Santorini Park guide](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/santorini-park-lovely-photo-spot-right-outside-hanoi). In season, the [Red River flower fields](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/red-river-dyke-flower-park-hot-spots-photography-camping-and-picnics-hanoi) are another easy option.

## How to choose

- **One day, big scenery:** Ninh Bình.
- **Short on time:** Bát Tràng or Santorini Park.
- **Photography:** Quảng Phú Cầu early in the day, or Ninh Bình in the soft late-afternoon light.
- **Bay views:** Hạ Long, ideally overnight.

## Practical tips

- **Start early.** Traffic leaving Hanoi builds quickly after 8am.
- **Avoid weekends and public holidays** for Ninh Bình, Hạ Long and the Perfume Pagoda if you can.
- **Bring cash** for entrance fees, boats and snacks.
- **Check the weather.** Rain makes boat rides and village paths less pleasant.
- **Grab or a private car** is easiest for Bát Tràng, Quảng Phú Cầu and Đường Lâm.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best day trip from Hanoi?
For most first-time visitors, Ninh Bình. The scenery is spectacular and it fits in one day.

### Is Hạ Long Bay worth a day trip from Hanoi?
It is possible, but you spend 5–6 hours on the road. An overnight cruise is more relaxing.

### What is the closest day trip to Hanoi?
Bát Tràng ceramic village, about 13 km away, roughly 30 minutes by car.

### Do I need a tour?
No. All seven are reachable by car, and Ninh Bình also by train. A tour helps with boats, tickets and timing.
