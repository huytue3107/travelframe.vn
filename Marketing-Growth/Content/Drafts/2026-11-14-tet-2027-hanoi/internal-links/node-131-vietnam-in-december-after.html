Vietnam in December: Where to Go From Hanoi (Sapa, Ha Long, Ninh Binh) and Where It's Warm

Vietnam in December is three countries in one: cool and dry in the north, still rainy in the center, hot and sunny in the south. Where to go from Hanoi, what to pack and when the weather works.

Vietnam stretches more than 1,600 km from north to south, and in December that distance really shows. Hanoi is in its cool, dry winter. Central Vietnam is still in the tail of its rainy season. The south is hot, sunny and in peak beach season.

The good news: wherever you start, there is somewhere with good weather. Here is the region-by-region picture, with Hanoi as your base. Last checked November 12, 2026.

The north: Vietnam's real winter

Hanoi

Cool, dry and decorated for Christmas and New Year. It's the most comfortable time of year to walk the Old Quarter. Full details in Hanoi weather in December. To walk it with a photographer-guide, browse our private tours of Hanoi.

Ninh Bình (about 2 hours by road)

Limestone karsts, rowing boats and temples. December is calm and comfortable, though the rice fields are bare after harvest. Pair it with the stone cathedral at Phát Diệm, which draws big crowds at Christmas. See day trips from Hanoi.

Hạ Long Bay (about 2.5–3 hours by road)

December means mist. The bay looks moody and beautiful, but fog can limit views and slow or delay cruises. The water is too cold for comfortable swimming, and it feels much colder on deck.

  • Choose a cruise with a flexible cancellation policy.
  • Bring a windproof jacket for the top deck.
  • Kayaking is fine on calm days. Swimming, less so.

Sapa and Hà Giang

Cold, clear on good days and often wrapped in fog. Days are often 5–15°C and nights can approach freezing. Frost is possible on cold nights, and light snow on the highest peaks, like Fansipan, is possible but rare. Trekking is open; pack thermals, gloves and waterproof boots.

Getting there: Sapa is about 5–6 hours by road from Hanoi, or an overnight train to Lào Cai plus a short transfer.

Central Vietnam: the wettest region in December

Huế, Hội An and Da Nang are at the end of their rainy season. December can bring days of heavy rain and, in some years, flooding in Hội An's old town. Temperatures are mild.

If central Vietnam is on your route, keep your plans flexible and save it for the end of the month. Or swap it for the north or the south and visit in spring instead.

The south: warm, dry and busy

Ho Chi Minh City and the Mekong Delta

The dry season has started. Expect hot, mostly sunny days. Christmas in Saigon is big, with crowds around Notre-Dame Cathedral and Nguyễn Huệ walking street.

Phú Quốc

December is peak beach season: calm seas, sunny skies and high hotel prices around Christmas and New Year. Flights from Hanoi take about two hours.

Three December routes starting in Hanoi

  1. Classic north (6–7 days): Hanoi 3 nights, Ninh Bình 1 night, Hạ Long Bay cruise 1–2 nights.
  2. Mountains and city (6 days): Hanoi 3 nights, Sapa 2–3 nights. Bring winter clothes.
  3. Winter and sun (7–8 days): Hanoi 3–4 nights for Christmas, then fly to Phú Quốc for New Year on the beach.

Spending Christmas in Hanoi? Try our 5-day Christmas and New Year itinerary. Traveling in February instead? Tết, the Lunar New Year, falls on February 6, 2027. Read Tet 2027 in Hanoi for what stays open that week.

What to pack for Vietnam in December

  • North: layers, a light down or windproof jacket, a scarf; add thermals and gloves for Sapa.
  • Center: a rain jacket and quick-dry shoes.
  • South: summer clothes, sunscreen and a hat.
  • Everywhere: modest clothes for temples and churches, and a few masks for hazy days in the cities.

Frequently asked questions

Is December a good time to visit Vietnam?

Yes, for the north and the south. Central Vietnam can be wet. Christmas and New Year are peak season, so book hotels and cruises early.

Where is warm in Vietnam in December?

The south: Ho Chi Minh City, the Mekong Delta and Phú Quốc are hot and sunny. Nha Trang and the central coast can still be rainy.

Does it snow in Vietnam in December?

Rarely, and only on the highest mountains in the far north, such as Fansipan near Sapa. Frost is more common than snow.

Is Ha Long Bay worth it in December?

Yes, if you like atmosphere. Fog makes it moody and photogenic, but views can be limited. Book a flexible cruise and dress warmly.
