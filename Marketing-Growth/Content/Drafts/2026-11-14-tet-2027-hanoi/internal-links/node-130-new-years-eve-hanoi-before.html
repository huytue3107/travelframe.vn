New Year's Eve in Hanoi 2027: Countdown Spots, Fireworks and Getting Home Safely

Hanoi rings in January 1 with a countdown at Hoan Kiem Lake and fireworks across the city. What happened last year, what to expect for 2027 and how to enjoy it without getting stuck.

Hanoi celebrates two New Years. The first is January 1, with a countdown and fireworks on the night of December 31. The second, much bigger one is Tết, the Lunar New Year, which falls on February 6 in 2027.

This guide is about December 31. As of November 12, 2026, the city has not announced its 2027 countdown or fireworks plan. Below is what happened last year, what usually repeats and how to plan your night. We will update this page when the official plan is out.

New Year's Eve 2026–2027 at a glance

  • Date: Thursday, December 31, 2026. January 1, 2027 is a Friday and a public holiday, so many locals get a three-day weekend.
  • Main hub: Hoàn Kiếm Lake and the Old Quarter.
  • Fireworks: last year, 15 minutes from 11:45pm to midnight at several sites around the city.
  • Traffic: streets around the lake close to vehicles from the evening. Walk.
  • Weather: around 15°C at night, sometimes with drizzle. Bring a jacket.

What happened last year (Dec 31, 2025)

Use this as a guide to what 2026 may look like. Exact sites and times can change.

  • Countdown: the city's official Countdown 2026 ran from 7pm to midnight at Đông Kinh Nghĩa Thục Square, at the north end of the lake, and across the lakeside walking zone.
  • Fireworks: Hanoi set off fireworks at 5 sites with 6 launch positions, from 11:45pm to midnight. Two were at Hoàn Kiếm Lake, in front of the Hà Nội Mới newspaper office and the Hanoi Post Office. The others were in Thống Nhất Park, along the old F1 track in Từ Liêm, at Lạc Long Quân flower garden in Tây Hồ and in Hà Đông.
  • A first: pyrotechnics were launched from the middle of Hoàn Kiếm Lake for the first time.
  • Music: a separate sponsored countdown with DJs took place at August Revolution Square, in front of the Opera House, from about 7:30pm.

Where to watch

Around Hoàn Kiếm Lake

The heart of the night. Both lake launch sites were on the shore, so most of the lake edge has a view. The north end, near Đông Kinh Nghĩa Thục Square, has the stage and the biggest crowd. The east and south shores are a little calmer.

A café or restaurant with a view

Balconies over the lake sell out early for December 31 and often charge a set menu or minimum spend. Book in advance and ask exactly what's included.

Away from the center

If you are staying in Tây Hồ, the Lạc Long Quân flower garden site was closer and calmer. Thống Nhất Park is a good option south of the center.

How to plan your night

  1. Eat early: have dinner by 8pm. Restaurants near the lake get packed.
  2. Arrive by 9–10pm: pick your spot and stay put. Moving around the lake after 11pm is slow.
  3. Know your exit: agree a meeting point in case you lose each other, as phone signal can slow down in big crowds.
  4. Getting home: walk a few blocks away from the lake before booking a Grab. Expect surge prices and a wait after midnight.
  5. Stay warm: you'll stand for hours. A jacket and a scarf make a big difference.
  6. Keep valuables close: phone and wallet in front pockets.

New Year's Day: what's open?

January 1 is a public holiday, but in Hanoi most restaurants, cafés, shops and sights stay open. Government offices and banks close. The walking zone around the lake is busy all weekend.

It's a lovely morning for a slow walk. The streets are quiet until late morning while the city sleeps in. Make it a slow photo tour of the city with a local guide.

Don't confuse it with Tết

If you are in Vietnam in early February 2027, you'll see the real New Year. Tết Eve falls on the night of February 5, 2027. It brings far bigger fireworks, many businesses closing for several days and a completely different, family-centered mood. Last Tết, Hanoi held fireworks at dozens of sites across the city.

Frequently asked questions

Are there fireworks in Hanoi on New Year's Eve?

In recent years, yes. For New Year 2026, Hanoi had fireworks at 5 sites from 11:45pm to midnight, including two at Hoàn Kiếm Lake. The 2027 plan is usually announced in mid to late December.

Where is the countdown in Hanoi?

The main official countdown has been held at Đông Kinh Nghĩa Thục Square and the walking streets around Hoàn Kiếm Lake.

Is New Year's Eve in Hanoi safe?

It is crowded but generally friendly and well policed. The main risks are getting separated, pickpocketing in dense crowds and slow transport home.

Is January 1 a holiday in Vietnam?

Yes, January 1 is a public holiday. In 2027 it is a Friday, so many people have a long weekend.
