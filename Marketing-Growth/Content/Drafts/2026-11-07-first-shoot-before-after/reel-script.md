# First pro shoot: minute 1 vs minute 30

## Hook (0-3s)

She only ever took selfies. This was her first real photoshoot.

## Beats

1. Minute 1. Arms stiff, eyes locked on the lens, a nervous laugh. Overlay: Minute 1.

2. Minute 10. One direction at a time: turn your back, touch your hair, soften the hand. Overlay: Minute 10.

3. Minute 30. She stops posing and starts moving. The photos change with her. Overlay: Minute 30.

4. The reveal. She sees the back of the camera and goes quiet, then laughs. Hold the final frame. Pro move: if you freeze, ask to see the screen after the first few shots.
