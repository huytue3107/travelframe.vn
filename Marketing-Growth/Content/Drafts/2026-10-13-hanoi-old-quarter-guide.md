# Hanoi Old Quarter Guide (2026): Map, Things to Do, Night Market and Where to Eat

*By TravelFrame Editorial Team · Updated October 2026*

Hanoi's Old Quarter is the compact grid of streets just north of Hoàn Kiếm Lake. For centuries it was the city's merchant district, and it is still where most visitors stay, eat and spend their first day. It can feel chaotic for the first hour. After that it becomes one of the most walkable, rewarding neighborhoods in Southeast Asia.

This guide covers how the quarter is laid out, what is worth your time, when the night market runs, where to eat, and how to get there from the airport.

## Where Is the Old Quarter? A Simple Mental Map

Think of the Old Quarter as a rough triangle:

- **South edge:** Hoàn Kiếm Lake. The easiest landmark, and where most walks begin.
- **North edge:** Đồng Xuân Market, the large covered market at the top of Hàng Đào street.
- **East edge:** the Long Biên Bridge approach and the dyke road along the Red River.
- **West edge:** Phùng Hưng street and the elevated railway arches.

Many streets still carry the name of the trade once practiced there: *Hàng* means "goods". Hàng Bạc was silver, Hàng Mã paper offerings, Hàng Thiếc tin. A few still sell what their name promises, which is part of the fun.

**Tip:** Save a few pins before you go (your hotel, Hoàn Kiếm Lake, Đồng Xuân Market, St. Joseph's Cathedral). Street names change at almost every junction, and a map app with offline maps is more useful than any paper map.

## Best Things to Do in the Old Quarter

### 1. Walk around Hoàn Kiếm Lake at sunrise
Locals exercise, dance and practice tai chi around the lake in the early morning. Cross the red Huc Bridge to [Ngọc Sơn Temple](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/ngoc-son-temple-renowned-spiritual-and-cultural-symbol-hanoi) before the tour groups arrive.

### 2. Get lost in the small lanes
The main streets are busy, but narrow alleys open into courtyards, family shrines and tiny cafés. Start with our [5 Old Quarter alleys to slow down in](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/5-old-quarter-alleys-slow-down).

### 3. Drink egg coffee where it was invented
Egg coffee is a Hanoi original. Read the story of [Café Giảng and how to photograph it](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/hanoi-egg-coffee-cafe-giang-photography-guide).

### 4. Visit Đồng Xuân Market
The city's largest covered market sells mostly wholesale goods: fabric, household items and dried foods. The street food stalls around its edges are the real draw.

### 5. See St. Joseph's Cathedral
The neo-Gothic [St. Joseph's Cathedral](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/st-josephs-cathedral) sits on the quarter's southwest edge, surrounded by cafés and lemon-tea stalls.

### 6. Spend an evening on Tạ Hiện
Tạ Hiện and the surrounding streets fill with low stools, beer and music after dark. Our guide: [Tạ Hiện street, where Hanoi nights come alive](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/ta-hien-street-where-hanoi-nights-come-alive).

### 7. Walk to the railway and Long Biên Bridge
Hanoi's railway heritage sits on the quarter's edges. Read the [Train Street safety guide](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/hanoi-train-street-2026-safety-first-guide-and-better-railway-photo-spots) before you go, and consider the calmer view from Long Biên Bridge.

## Hanoi Old Quarter Night Market

On weekend evenings, the street running north from Hoàn Kiếm Lake along Hàng Đào toward Đồng Xuân Market closes to traffic and becomes a walking night market. Expect clothing, souvenirs, snacks and crowds. Around the lake, the walking-street area also closes to traffic on weekend evenings, with street performers and families out in force.

- **When:** Friday to Sunday evenings. *(Re-check current hours before publishing.)*
- **Tips:** Go early in the evening, keep your phone in a front pocket, and bargain politely at clothing stalls.

## How to Get from Hanoi Airport to the Old Quarter

Nội Bài International Airport is roughly 30 km north of the Old Quarter.

- **Ride-hailing app (Grab, Xanh SM):** the simplest door-to-door option. Check the fare in the app before confirming.
- **Airport bus 86:** a public bus linking the airport with the Old Quarter area and the railway station. Cheaper and slower. *(Re-check route and fare before publishing.)*
- **Hotel transfer:** many Old Quarter hotels arrange pickup. Confirm the price in writing.

Allow 45–60 minutes by car, more at rush hour.

## Where to Stay

Staying inside or on the edge of the Old Quarter puts you within walking distance of the lake, the food and the night market. Light sleepers should ask for a room away from the street, or stay near the lake or in the French Quarter just south.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many days do you need in the Old Quarter?**
One full day covers the highlights. Two days let you slow down, add the French Quarter and enjoy the evenings.

**Is the Hanoi Old Quarter safe?**
It is generally safe for visitors, including solo travelers. The main risks are traffic and petty theft in crowds. Watch your phone and bag at night markets.

**What is the best time of day to explore?**
Early morning, for quiet streets and soft light around the lake, and early evening, when the food stalls open.

**Is the Old Quarter walkable?**
Yes. Hoàn Kiếm Lake to Đồng Xuân Market is about a 15-minute walk without stops.
