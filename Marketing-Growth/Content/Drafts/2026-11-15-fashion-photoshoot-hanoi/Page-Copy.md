# Fashion Photoshoot in Hanoi: Editorial Looks in the Old Quarter

Some travelers pack outfits with the photos already in mind. If that's you, a regular photo walk may feel too loose.

A fashion photoshoot in Hanoi plans each look around a backdrop: a market sign, a metal shutter, a cyclo, a lit street at night. This guide covers how to plan your looks, where we shoot, how the posing works, and what it costs.

## Quick answer

- **Where:** Đồng Xuân Market and the Old Quarter streets around it. Tràng Tiền and the French Quarter after dark.
- **Plan ahead:** send 2 or 3 reference photos and tell us what you will wear.
- **Looks:** plan them with us before the shoot. We confirm how many fit your time in writing.
- **Etiquette:** we ask shop owners and drivers before using their shopfronts, motorbikes or cyclos.
- **Cost with us:** Premium for 2 hours is 120 USD. VIP for 2 hours is 198 USD and includes an outfit.

## Plan your looks

Start with the backdrop, then pick the outfit. A look works when it stands apart from what is behind it.

| Look | Backdrop that suits it | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A bright dress | Silver shutters, pale walls | The color carries the frame |
| Streetwear | Parked motorbikes, a cyclo | Everyday street details match the mood |
| Dark clothes | Shop lights and traffic at night | Your outline stays clear against bright windows |
| White or pale | Steel frames of Long Biên Bridge | Soft fabric against rough metal |

One outfit can be enough. Change your framing, your pose and where you look, and one look gives you a full set.

Pro move: bring one accessory that changes the mood fast, such as sunglasses, a hat or a jacket.

## Where we shoot

### Đồng Xuân Market

The market sign, stacked goods and stairways give you a clear sense of place. We shoot outside and around the market. Inside, only where stall owners say yes.

### Shutters and yellow walls

Old Quarter shutters have lines, scratches and handwritten marks. Yellow walls and green shutters add warm color. Both make good plain backgrounds for a strong look.

### Motorbikes and cyclos

They say Hanoi in one frame. We always ask the owner or the driver first, and we move on if they say no.

### Hanoi after dark

Around Tràng Tiền, buses, motorbikes and shop windows light the frame for you. The streets keep moving, and that movement ends up in the photos.

### Long Biên Bridge

The rusted steel gives a strong, graphic background. We shoot from the pedestrian walkway only.

We don't shoot on the Train Street rails. See our [Train Street safety guide](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/hanoi-train-street-2026-safety-first-guide-and-better-railway-photo-spots).

## Posing with direction

On a fashion shoot we direct more than on a photo walk. You get strong poses, and the moments between them.

- **Arms out against a shutter.** A simple shape that fills the frame.
- **A look back over your shoulder,** with the market sign still in view.
- **Leaning on a pole with arms folded,** while the traffic passes behind you.
- **A small adjustment,** like fixing your glasses or your collar, caught mid-move.

We show you the shots as we go, so you can see what is working.

## Changing between looks

Wear your first look and bring the others in a small bag. Tell us when you book, and we plan where you can change.

Wear comfortable shoes for walking between spots, and bring heels for the shots if your look needs them.
