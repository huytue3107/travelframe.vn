Hoàn Kiếm Lake is the centre of Hanoi in every sense. The Old Quarter starts at its northern edge, the French Quarter spreads south and east, and almost every first day in the city passes along its shore. It is small, about 12 hectares, so you can walk all the way round in roughly half an hour.

The lake is free and open all the time. What changes is the mood: tai chi, dancing and jogging at dawn, a quieter shore through the day, and on weekend evenings the roads around it close to traffic and fill with families and street performers.

Quick answer

  • Where: central Hanoi, between the Old Quarter and the French Quarter, ringed by Đinh Tiên Hoàng, Lê Thái Tổ and Hàng Khay streets.
  • Cost: the lake is free. Ngọc Sơn Temple, on the island at the north end, costs 50,000 VND for adults.
  • Walking street: from 19:00 on Friday to midnight on Sunday, the roads around the lake close to cars and motorbikes.
  • Best time: 5:30–7:00 for locals exercising and soft light; blue hour for the lit red bridge.
  • How long: 30–40 minutes for a loop, 1–2 hours with the temple and a coffee.

The legend behind the name

Hoàn Kiếm means "Returned Sword". In the 15th century, under Ming occupation, Lê Lợi led the uprising that drove the Ming out, and he became emperor in 1428. Legend says he fought with a magic sword called Thuận Thiên, "Heaven's Will". Boating on the lake after the war, he met a golden turtle that rose from the water and took the sword back to its divine owner. He renamed the lake to mark the moment.

The turtle is not only folklore. Real giant soft-shell turtles lived in the lake, and Ngọc Sơn Temple displays two preserved specimens.

What to see around the lake

Turtle Tower (Tháp Rùa)

The small three-storey tower on its own islet was built between 1884 and 1886 to recall the legend. You cannot visit it, only look at it from the shore, which is how most people photograph it.

The Húc Bridge and Ngọc Sơn Temple

The red wooden bridge at the north end crosses to Ngọc Sơn Temple on Jade Island. Buy the 50,000 VND ticket before you cross. The temple usually opens from about 7:00 to 19:00, with later closing on weekend evenings. We cover it in detail in our Ngọc Sơn Temple guide.

The weekend walking street

Since 2016, the streets around the lake have been pedestrian-only from 19:00 on Friday until midnight on Sunday. Expect folk games, live music, dance groups and snack stalls, plus thick crowds after 20:00. The neighbouring Old Quarter night market runs on the same evenings. During national holidays the city sometimes adds extra nights.

Photo tips

  • Dawn: the best time for people photos. Locals exercise in groups on the paths; smile and ask before shooting close.
  • Turtle Tower: walk the shore until the tower lines up against trees, not buildings. A 70–135 mm lens fills the frame from the bank.
  • The Húc Bridge: shoot from the shore just after sunset, when the bridge lights come on but the sky still has colour.
  • Crowds: weekday mornings are calm. Weekend evenings are lively but hard for clean frames.

FAQ

Is Hoan Kiem Lake free to visit?

Yes. Walking around the lake is free at any time. Only Ngọc Sơn Temple charges a ticket, 50,000 VND for adults.

When is the Hoan Kiem walking street open?

From 19:00 on Friday to midnight on Sunday every week, with extra nights on some national holidays. Cars and motorbikes are not allowed then.

Can you visit Turtle Tower?

No. It stands on a small islet and is not open to the public. You see and photograph it from the shore.

How long does it take to walk around Hoan Kiem Lake?

About 30–40 minutes at an easy pace. Allow longer if you stop at the temple or for photos.

What is the best time to visit?

Early morning, 5:30–7:00, for local life and soft light. Visit on a weekend evening if you want the walking-street atmosphere.
