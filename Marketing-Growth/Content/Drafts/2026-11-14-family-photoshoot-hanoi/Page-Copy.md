# Family and Group Photoshoot in Hanoi: How It Works and What It Costs

Every group trip has a photographer, and that person is missing from almost every photo. A family or group photoshoot in Hanoi fixes that. Someone else holds the camera, and everyone gets to be in the pictures.

This guide covers how a group session runs, how to pace it for kids and grandparents, where to shoot, and how the price works.

## Quick answer

- **Where:** Hoàn Kiếm Lake and the Old Quarter. Paths are flat and cafés are close by for breaks.
- **How long:** one hour for one spot, two hours for two or three spots with a break.
- **When:** early morning, when the streets are quieter. Late afternoon also works for groups of friends.
- **What to wear:** two or three coordinated colors, not identical outfits.
- **Cost with us:** the tier price covers 2 guests, then 15 USD per extra guest per hour. Four people for 2 hours on Premium is 180 USD.

## How a group session runs

1. **Everyone together first.** While energy is high, we do the whole-group shots.
2. **Then smaller groups.** Parents and kids, siblings, best friends, couples.
3. **Then portraits.** A few solo shots for anyone who wants them.
4. **Candids all the way through.** Most of the good ones happen while you walk, chat and wait to cross the street.

Pro move: give the camera-shy person a job, like leading the walk or carrying the flowers. They forget about the camera.

## Pacing it for kids and grandparents

- **Keep it short.** For young kids, one to two hours is plenty.
- **Plan a café stop.** Halfway through, sit down, order a drink and keep shooting at the table.
- **Mind the pavements.** Old Quarter pavements are uneven and often full of parked motorbikes. A carrier is easier than a stroller for small children.
- **Let the slowest walker set the pace.** Tell us ages and any walking needs when you book, and we plan the route around them.

## Where to shoot in Hanoi

### Hoàn Kiếm Lake

Benches, old trees and open paths. Easy for all ages, with space for a group to stand together.

### Old Quarter cafés and lanes

Café windows, yellow walls and quiet corners. Good for small groups and pairs.

### St. Joseph's Cathedral

A big, recognisable backdrop for a whole-group photo. After dark it is lit up.

### After dark, for groups of friends

Lantern streets and lit cafés suit a night out. We skip the Train Street rails for every group. See our [Train Street safety guide](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/hanoi-train-street-2026-safety-first-guide-and-better-railway-photo-spots).

## What to wear as a group

- Pick two or three colors that sit well together, such as white, beige and one accent.
- Avoid big logos and busy prints that pull the eye.
- Comfortable shoes for everyone. You will walk between spots.

## FAQ

**Our kids won't sit still. Is that a problem?**
No. Moving kids make great candid photos. We keep the posed group shot short.

**Can we split into smaller groups?**
Yes. We plan time for pairs and portraits after the group shots.

**Can grandparents join for part of the walk?**
Yes. Start with the whole family by the lake, then let them rest at a café while the rest of the group carries on.

**When do we get the photos?**
All original photos within 24 hours. Edited photos and video within 48 hours.
