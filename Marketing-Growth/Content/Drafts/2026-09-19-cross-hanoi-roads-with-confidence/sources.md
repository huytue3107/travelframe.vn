## Production guardrails

- Film at a controlled, clearly visible crossing point.
- Do not ask talent to enter a dangerous traffic gap for the shot.
- Do not use wording such as "traffic will go around you" or "just keep
  walking."
- Keep the final line: if the crossing is unclear, do not force it.
