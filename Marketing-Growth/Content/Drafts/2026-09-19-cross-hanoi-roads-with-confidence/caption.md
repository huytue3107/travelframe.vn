Hanoi traffic looks impossible until you know what to watch for.

Start at a clear, visible crossing point.
Use signals or marked crossings when they are available.
Look both ways and wait for a gap you can actually read.
Once you move, keep an even pace and avoid sudden direction changes.

Most importantly: never force a crossing that still feels unclear. Wait, choose
a quieter point, or cross with local support.
