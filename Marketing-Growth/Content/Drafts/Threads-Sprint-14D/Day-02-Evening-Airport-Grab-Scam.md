### [PART 1/5: ROOT HOOK]
A GUY LANDS AT NỘI BÀI, WALKS OUT OF ARRIVALS, AND SOMEONE IN A GREEN GRAB JACKET OFFERS TO WALK HIM TO HIS CAR.

It happens dozens of times every single evening at Terminal 2.

A tired traveler steps out of the automatic sliding glass doors into Hanoi's humid night air. A polite young man in a green helmet and jacket approaches, smiles, points at a Grab logo on his smartphone screen, and says: "Grab? Where are you going?"

Here is why you must never follow him:

---

### [PART 2/5: THE MECHANICS OF THE TRAP]
This driver does NOT have your booked ride.

He is an unaffiliated street tout running what locals call the "Grab Fake" hustle. If you show him your phone, he will quickly tap your screen, cancel your active booking in the app, or claim that "the app has technical server errors right now, but I can take you for the exact same fare."

Once you reach his unmarked private sedan parked in the distant secondary parking lot, the story changes.

---

### [PART 3/5: THE FARE HIKE AT THE DESTINATION]
When you arrive in the Old Quarter 45 minutes later, he will demand 650,000 to 800,000 VND ($26 to $32 USD) in cash, plus extra charges for airport tolls and luggage handling.

If you protest, he points at an unofficial paper rate card taped inside the glove compartment. Because you have no ride receipt inside the official app, you have zero recourse.

---

### [PART 4/5: THE GOLDEN GROUND RULE FOR NỘI BÀI]
Here is the simple rule that completely protects you:

(1) Never speak to anyone calling out "Grab taxi" inside the terminal.
(2) Walk out to Lane E / T2 ride hailing pickup bays on the 1st floor.
(3) Open your own Grab or Xanh SM app, check the assigned 5 digit license plate, and ONLY get in when the plate on the bumper matches your screen 100%.

The standard honest ride from Nội Bài to Hoàn Kiếm costs 280,000 to 340,000 VND (including the 15,000 VND airport toll).
