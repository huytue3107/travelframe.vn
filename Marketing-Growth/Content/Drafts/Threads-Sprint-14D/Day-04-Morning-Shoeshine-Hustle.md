# TravelFrame Threads Sprint — Day 4 Ca Sáng (07:30 GMT+7)
## Pillar 4: Scam Teardowns & "Story Time" Tales (Pattern P-042)

- **Mã bài đăng:** `TH-SPRINT-D04-AM`
- **Khung giờ đăng:** 07:30 - 08:30 AM GMT+7
- **Chủ đề:** Bóc mẽ chiêu trò đánh giày vỉa hè phố cổ Hà Nội
- **Trạng thái:** Ready for Review / Staged

---

### [PART 1/5: ROOT HOOK]
THE OLD QUARTER SHOESHINE HUSTLE HAS RUN ON THE EXACT SAME 3 MOVES FOR A DECADE.

You are sitting on a low plastic stool outside a cafe near Hoan Kiem lake, sipping an iced coffee, completely relaxed.

A guy walking with a wooden tool basket stops right in front of your feet.

Within 8 seconds, you could be pressured out of $20 for a service you never asked for.

Here is the exact playbook so you never fall for it:

---

### [PART 2/5: MOVE 1 — THE PANIC POINT]
Move 1: The Panic Point.

The shoeshine guy points at your sneakers with genuine horror in his eyes. He shakes his head, touches the toe of your shoe, and says "Broken! Very dirty, glue broken!"

He makes you think your sole is peeling off and your shoe will fall apart right there on the sidewalk.

First time visitors immediately look down in confusion. That hesitation is the trap.

---

### [PART 3/5: MOVE 2 — THE FLIP FLOP SWAP]
Move 2: The Fast Swap.

Before you can answer, he drops a pair of cheap rubber sandals at your feet and literally slips your sneaker off your foot.

If you are wearing sandals, he squirts instant foam cleaner directly onto the leather without asking.

Now you are sitting barefoot in one shoe while he carries your sneaker 3 meters away and starts scrubbing with steel wool or rapid fire brushes.

---

### [PART 4/5: MOVE 3 — THE SURPRISE SURGERY]
Move 3: The Surprise Surgery.

Five minutes later, he brings your shoe back. It is shiny, but he also points proudly to a thick black rubber pad he just superglued onto your sole.

You never agreed to a resole.

He pulls out a laminated cardboard slip or shows a calculator screen demanding 400,000 to 600,000 VND ($16 to $24 USD). If you push back, he acts deeply offended and makes a loud scene.

---

### [PART 5/5: THE DEFENSE & TRAVELFRAME BRIDGE]
How to handle it smoothly:

Look straight ahead, give a firm smile, shake your head, and say "No, thank you" while pulling your feet back under your stool before he can touch your footwear. Once he sees you know the drill, he moves on immediately.
