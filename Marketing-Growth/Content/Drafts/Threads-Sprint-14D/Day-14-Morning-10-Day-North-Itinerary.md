### [PART 1/5: ROOT HOOK]
A 10 DAY NORTHERN VIETNAM ITINERARY THAT WILL NOT LEAVE YOU TRAVEL BURNT.

Most commercial agencies sell exhausting package routes: five provinces in seven days, 16 hours trapped in sleeper buses, and checking into a new hotel every single night.

You return home feeling like you need another vacation just to recover.

Here is the realistic, slow travel 10 day itinerary designed for deep memories and zero burnout:

---

### [PART 2/5: DAYS 1 TO 3 — HANOI IMMERSION]
Days 1 to 3: The Slow Heartbeat of Hanoi.

Give the capital three full days:

Day 1: Recover from jet lag, master crossing the street, and sip egg coffee on an alley balcony.

Day 2: 6 AM sunrise walk around Hoan Kiem lake, French quarter architecture, and an authentic street food crawl.

Day 3: Sunset on Long Bien bridge, vintage vinyl cafes, and midnight pho on Hang Chieu.

Stay in one central Old Quarter boutique hotel.

---

### [PART 3/5: DAYS 4 TO 6 — NINH BINH KARSTS]
Days 4 to 6: Ninh Binh (The Karsts Without The Fatigue).

Just a comfortable two hour private drive south of Hanoi:

Base yourself in a tranquil eco lodge in Trang An or Tam Coc.

Rent a bicycle and pedal past lotus ponds between giant limestone peaks.

Take a wooden sampan boat rowed with feet through water caves. You get jaw dropping Halong Bay style scenery with zero ocean swells and zero crowded cruise ships.

---

### [PART 4/5: DAYS 7 TO 10 — THE MOUNTAIN PLATEAU]
Days 7 to 10: Bac Kan or Ha Giang Plateau.

Head north into the dramatic limestone plateaus:

Choose Ba Be National Park for pristine emerald lakes and bamboo kayaking, or the Ha Giang Dong Van plateau for breathtaking mountain passes.

Stay in traditional Tay or Hmong wooden homestays. Share family dinners, wake up to rooster crows in the valley, and witness untouched rural life.
