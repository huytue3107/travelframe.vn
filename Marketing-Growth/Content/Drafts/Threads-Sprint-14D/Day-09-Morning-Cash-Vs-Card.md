### [PART 2/5: THE TWO PAYMENT REALITIES]
The Split Reality of Vietnamese Commerce:

High end hotels, craft cocktail lounges, supermarket chains, and ride hailing apps like Grab accept international Visa and Mastercard seamlessly.

However, Hanoi's true heart lives on the street:

The grandmother serving Michelin recognized Bun Cha, the 80 year old egg coffee counter, and the neighborhood fruit lady only accept cash or domestic QR transfers.

---

### [PART 3/5: THE VIETQR TRAP]
Why you cannot just scan the QR codes:

Every street cart in Hanoi displays a blue VietQR code standee on their table. Locals tap their banking app, scan in two seconds, and walk away.

However, these QR codes connect exclusively to the Vietnamese National Payment Gateway. Foreign banking apps like Chase, Monzo, or Revolut cannot process them.

Unless you hold a local bank account, that QR standee does not help you.

---

### [PART 4/5: THE 500K DAILY CASH RULE]
How experienced travelers manage daily spending:

Keep your credit card locked safely in your hotel room for large bookings and Grab rides.

Withdraw 1,500,000 to 2,000,000 VND (roughly $60 to $80 USD) from local ATMs (TPBank and VPBank charge zero or minimal local fees).

Ask the teller or ATM for mixed smaller denominations (50,000 and 100,000 VND) so you always have exact change on the street.
