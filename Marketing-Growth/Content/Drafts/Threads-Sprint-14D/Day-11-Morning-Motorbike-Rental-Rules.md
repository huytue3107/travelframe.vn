### [PART 2/5: THE INSURANCE VOID TRAP]
The Legal and Insurance Trap:

To legally ride a motorcycle over 50cc in Vietnam, you must carry an International Driving Permit (IDP) issued under the 1968 Vienna Convention with an active motorcycle endorsement.

If you only hold a regular car driver license or a 1949 Geneva permit (like US and Canadian permits), you are technically driving unlicensed.

If an accident occurs, your international travel medical insurance will deny coverage completely.

---

### [PART 3/5: THE 4 POINT BIKE INSPECTION]
What to check before handing over cash:

(1) Brakes: Squeeze both levers hard while rolling. Spongy brakes will fail on steep mountain descents.
(2) Tires: Inspect the rubber tread. Worn bald tires on wet limestone mountain asphalt will slip in seconds.
(3) Helmet: Reject thin toy plastic caps. Ask for a solid certified three quarter helmet with a secure strap.
(4) Lights and Horn: Test high beams and the horn, your primary safety signal.

---

### [PART 4/5: THE ROAD TONNAGE LAW]
The Unwritten Law of Tonnage:

On Vietnamese roads, size dictates right of way:

Container trucks, sleeper buses, and construction dump trucks cannot stop quickly on mountain bends.

Never attempt to overtake a truck on an inside hairpin turn.

Keep your speed below 40 km/h, use your horn before entering blind corners, and always yield to larger vehicles without hesitation.
