---

### [PART 2/5: THE PRICE GAP]
The Airport Markup vs Downtown Reality:

At the airport arrivals hall, tourist packages are priced between $15 and $25 USD for a 15 to 30 day physical SIM card.

If you walk into an official carrier store in Hanoi (like Viettel or Vinaphone), the exact same registered high speed data package costs roughly 120,000 to 150,000 VND (about $5 to $6 USD).

You are paying a 300% convenience markup just to have signal during your 45 minute taxi ride.

---

### [PART 3/5: THE UNLIMITED TRAP]
The "Unlimited" Fine Print:

Almost no prepaid tourist SIM in Vietnam provides truly unlimited high speed data.

In reality, 90% of airport packages give you 2GB to 4GB of daily high speed LTE data. Once you exceed that limit while watching reels or backing up photos, your speed drops to an excruciating 128 kbps (basically 1999 dial up speed).

You can barely load a map pin, let alone hail a Grab during rush hour.

---

### [PART 4/5: THE 2 SMARTEST ALTERNATIVES]
How savvy travelers handle internet in Vietnam:

(1) Buy an eSIM online before boarding your flight (via Airalo, Nomad, or Klook) for roughly $4 to $7 USD. Scan the QR code, activate it the second your plane touches down, and enjoy instant data.

(2) If your phone requires a physical SIM, connect to free airport WiFi, take your Grab to the hotel, and visit an official Viettel store on Trang Tien street with your passport.
