### [PART 2/5: THE GOLD STREET PHENOMENON]
Welcome to Pho Ha Trung (Ha Trung Street):

Located on the western edge of the Old Quarter, this single 200 meter lane is famous across Northern Vietnam for gold trading and foreign currency exchange.

Licensed gold merchants like Quoc Trinh and Quang Huy handle immense transaction volumes daily. Because of this liquidity, their cash exchange rates sit within 0.2% of the live international interbank rate.

---

### [PART 3/5: RULE 1 & 2 — BANKNOTE CONDITION]
Rule 1: Bring crisp, unbent, modern bills.

Vietnamese gold shops inspect cash under bright magnifying loupes:

(1) Only bring post 2013 $100 USD bills (the series featuring the 3D blue security ribbon).
(2) Zero tears, zero pen marks, and zero folded creases. Even a tiny 1 millimeter pinhole or faint stamp will cause the shop to reject the note or discount it by 3%.

Keep your large foreign bills completely flat inside a hard passport sleeve.

---

### [PART 4/5: RULE 3 — COUNTER ETIQUETTE]
Rule 2: Compare two shops and count before stepping away.

Walk into Quoc Trinh at 27 Ha Trung, ask the teller for their rate, then check the shop directly next door.

Type the amount on your phone calculator to eliminate language confusion.

When handed your stack of Vietnamese Dong notes, step to the side counter and count through the notes calmly under the glass lighting before walking out onto the sidewalk.
