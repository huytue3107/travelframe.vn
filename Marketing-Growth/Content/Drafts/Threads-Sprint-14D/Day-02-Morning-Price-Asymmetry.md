### [PART 1/7: ROOT HOOK]
6 THINGS THAT ARE ACTUALLY CHEAPER IN HANOI THAN YOU ASSUME, AND 6 THAT ARE PRICIER.

Hanoi has one of the weirdest price spectrums in Southeast Asia. You can live like royalty on $25 a day, or watch $120 vanish by lunchtime on things that locals pay pennies for.

Here is the exact price asymmetry breakdown from our local field notebooks:

1/7

---

### [PART 2/7: THE SHOCKINGLY CHEAP (PART 1)]
Things that are cheaper than you expect:

1. GrabBike rides under 3km cost 15,000 to 25,000 VND (around $0.60 to $1.00 USD). Zipping past gridlocked traffic on the back of a scooter is literally cheaper than a single NYC subway swipe.

2. A steaming bowl of pho bo with tender beef flank tucked in an Old Quarter residential alleyway costs 40,000 to 50,000 VND ($1.60 to $2.00 USD).

2/7

---

### [PART 3/7: THE SHOCKINGLY CHEAP (PART 2)]
3. Sidewalk iced black coffee ("cà phê đen đá") or fresh jasmine iced tea ("trà đá") on a street corner: 15,000 to 20,000 VND ($0.60 to $0.80 USD) for a drink plus free people watching.

4. Fresh laundry service: 25,000 VND ($1.00 USD) per kilo washed, dried, and folded within 6 hours.

5. Fresh tropical fruit peeled and sliced on the spot: 30,000 to 40,000 VND ($1.20 to $1.60 USD) for a giant bag of dragon fruit or ripe mango.

3/7

---

### [PART 4/7: THE UNEXPECTEDLY PRICEY (PART 1)]
Now, things that are surprisingly expensive if you don't watch out:

1. Any restaurant displaying a laminated English menu stand right in front of St. Joseph's Cathedral or Hoàn Kiếm Lake. You easily pay 160,000 to 220,000 VND ($6.50 to $9.00 USD) for a bland bowl of spring rolls.

2. Metered street taxis hailed right outside 5 star Old Quarter hotel lobbies. Many unmetered private cabs run fast meters that click at 2x the standard Vinasun or Mai Linh rate.

4/7

---

### [PART 5/7: THE UNEXPECTEDLY PRICEY (PART 2)]
3. Imported wine and foreign craft beers in western bars: 180,000 to 250,000 VND ($7 to $10 USD) per glass, often more than dinner for two at a local spot.

4. Laundry done directly through high end hotel laundry bags (charged per individual garment at 60,000 VND per shirt vs 25,000 VND for a whole kilo 50 meters down the street).

5. Buying souvenirs inside the night market without walking three stalls deeper into the alley to compare base prices.

5/7

---

### [PART 6/7: THE 5-DAY MATH]
The financial gap between navigating Hanoi with ground knowledge versus falling into tourist convenience traps is roughly $80 to $150 USD across a 5 day holiday.

That difference is enough to fund your entire northern mountain excursion or upgrade your entire stay.

6/7
