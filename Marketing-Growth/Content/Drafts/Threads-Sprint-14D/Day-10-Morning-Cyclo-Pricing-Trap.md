### [PART 1/5: ROOT HOOK]
THE HANOI CYCLO PRICING TRAP: HOW A "100K" RIDE BECOMES A $50 SHAKEDOWN.

Riding a three wheeled pedicab cyclo through the tree lined boulevards around Hoan Kiem lake is on almost every traveler's bucket list.

It is charming, breezy, and nostalgic.

However, if you do not lock in the exact pricing terms before sitting down, you could face an aggressive sidewalk shakedown at the end of your trip.

Here is how the trick works:

---

### [PART 2/5: THE AMBIGUOUS VERBAL DEAL]
The Ambiguous Agreement:

You wave down a cyclo driver on Dinh Tien Hoang street and ask "How much for a tour around the lake?"

The driver smiles warmly, holds up one index finger, and says "One hundred!"

You naturally assume he means 100,000 VND (roughly $4 USD) for the ride. You step into the cushioned carriage and enjoy the breeze. That ambiguity is where the trap begins.

---

### [PART 3/5: THE MULTIPLICATION AMBUSH]
The Surprise Invoice:

Thirty minutes later, the driver stops near St. Joseph Cathedral. You pull out a crisp 100,000 VND note and hand it over.

His friendly smile instantly vanishes. He crosses his arms, shakes his head angrily, and points at his watch:

"No! 100,000 VND per person, per 15 minutes! Two people, 30 minutes, 400,000 VND!"

Some aggressive drivers will even demand $50 USD, relying on tourist shock to force payment.

---

### [PART 4/5: THE 4 STEP DEFENSE]
How to take a cyclo with zero stress:

(1) Confirm the price is for the entire vehicle, not per person.
(2) Agree on a set duration in minutes or a specific destination.
(3) Type the exact number (e.g. "150,000 VND") on your phone calculator screen and have the driver nod clearly.
(4) Alternatively, book through reputable licensed companies like Sans Souci or ask your hotel concierge to hail one.
