### [PART 1/6: ROOT HOOK]
SKIP THE $18 ROOFTOP COCKTAILS: THE BEST SUNSET IN HANOI IS ON A 120 YEAR OLD RUSTING BRIDGE.

Many travelers pay steep cover charges for hotel rooftop terraces where you are trapped behind smudged safety glass surrounded by selfie sticks.

It costs zero dollars.

Here is how to experience Long Bien bridge at dusk:

---

### [PART 2/6: THE HISTORIC GIANT]
Long Bien Bridge was designed by French engineers Daydé and Pillé and completed in 1902.

It stretches over two kilometers across the Red River like a massive rusted iron dragon.

During the late afternoon, its weathered metal girders catch the setting sun, turning deep copper orange against the purple sky.

Beneath the bridge lies Bai Giua, an immense green island filled with wild banana plantations and vegetable farms.

---

### [PART 3/6: THE SENSORY RUSH]
The magic happens between 5:15 PM and 5:45 PM.

You lean against the century old iron railing as a thousand commuter motorbikes buzz past in the twilight.

Every 40 minutes, a slow diesel train crawls across the single central track. The wooden rail ties groan, the steel girders shudder under your boots, and the low whistle echoes across the open river valley. It gives you chills.

---

### [PART 4/6: BRIDGE STREET FOOD]
As darkness falls, elderly vendors set up tiny makeshift braziers right on the pedestrian walkway.

They fan glowing coals with woven bamboo fans, roasting sweet corn on the cob, purple sweet potatoes, and dried squid.

Grab an ear of roasted corn with chili scallion oil for 15,000 VND (60 cents), sit on the curb, and watch the distant city skyline light up like gold embers.

---

### [PART 5/6: HOW TO ACCESS IT SAFELY]
How to get onto the pedestrian path:

(1) Take a Grab bike to the ramp at Tran Nhat Duat street near the Old Quarter edge.
(2) Walk up the dedicated pedestrian walkway on the right hand side of the bridge.
(3) Do not climb onto the live railway track in the center when trains are approaching. Keep your phone firmly in hand and take your time walking to the middle span.
