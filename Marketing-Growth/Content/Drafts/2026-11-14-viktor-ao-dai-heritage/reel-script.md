# First time in an áo dài? 4 things that make the photos

## Hook (0-3s)

First time wearing an áo dài in Hanoi? Four small things make the photos.

## Beats

1. Pick a colour your backdrop doesn't have. Red stands out in the busy Old Quarter. Deep blue looks calm by the lake.

2. Walk slowly. The front panel moves with every step. That movement is the photo.

3. Sitting down? Move the back panel to the side first, so it doesn't crease. Pro move: wear flat sandals. You will walk more than you think.

4. Give your hands a job. A fan, a hat or a few flowers. At temples, ask the caretakers before you take photos.
