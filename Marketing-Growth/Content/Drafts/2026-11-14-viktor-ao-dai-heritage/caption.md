First time wearing an áo dài in Hanoi? Four small things make the photos. 👗

1. Pick a colour your backdrop doesn't have. Red for the Old Quarter, deep blue for the lake.
2. Walk slowly and let the front panel move.
3. Sitting down? Move the back panel to the side first.
4. Give your hands a job: a fan, a hat, a few flowers.

Pro move: wear flat sandals. You will walk more than you think.
