# Reel — Day 6 in Hanoi and you can't face another noodle

## Production brief
- Format: 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps, 33–36s. Subtitle do pipeline render.
- Voice: giọng kể chuyện hài nhẹ, tự giễu (warm local friend). Nhạc nền upbeat không lời, đổi nhạc nhẹ ở mỗi khu.

Day six in Hanoi, and you cannot face another noodle?

## Beats
**Beat 1 (3-10s) — Old Quarter.**
Stay right where you are.
Wood-fired pizza and Indian curry are inside the Old Quarter.

**Beat 2 (10-17s) — Linh Lang and Kim Mã.**
For sushi and ramen, go west to Hanoi's little Japantown.
Come after dark, when the izakaya lights come on.

**Beat 3 (17-24s) — My Dinh K-Town.**
Korean BBQ? Head to My Dinh.
About a kilometer of Korean restaurants, best with a hungry group.

**Beat 4 (24-30s) — West Lake.**
And for a long Western brunch, West Lake.
Pro move: too tired to go out? Grab Food delivers most of these to your hotel.
