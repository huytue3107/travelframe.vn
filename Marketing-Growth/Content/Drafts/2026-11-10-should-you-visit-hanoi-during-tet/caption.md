Everyone says don't visit Vietnam during Tết. Here's the local answer: come, just plan around three phases. 🏮

Before Tết: flower markets and kumquat trees on scooters. Our favourite photo week. [VERIFY]
New Year's Eve: crowds and fireworks near the lake. Plan your ride home early. [VERIFY]
Days one to three: quiet streets, many family shops closed, some holiday surcharges. Book ahead. [VERIFY]

Tết 2027 falls in early February. Check the exact date before you book flights. [VERIFY]
