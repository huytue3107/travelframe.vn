# Should you visit Hanoi during Tết 2027?

## Hook (0-3s)

Everyone says don't visit Vietnam during Tết. Here's the local answer.

## Beats

1. Come the week before. Flower markets, kumquat trees on scooters, the whole city shopping. The best photo week of the year. [VERIFY]

2. New Year's Eve is for the streets. Fireworks and crowds around the lake. Plan your ride home early. [VERIFY]

3. Days one to three are quiet. Many family shops close and some services charge a holiday surcharge. Book food and transport ahead. [VERIFY]

4. Tết 2027 falls in early February. Check the exact date before you book. [VERIFY]
