First omakase in Hanoi? Don't be the guest the chef remembers 🍣

The trap: you book the counter, then treat it like a normal dinner.
The move:
⏰ Arrive early. Many counters serve everyone together.
📞 Mention allergies when you book, not at the counter.
🥢 Go easy on soy sauce. Most pieces are already seasoned.
📸 No flash. Shoot from your seat, then eat. Ask before you film the chef.
