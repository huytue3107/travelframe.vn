# Seed comments — How a Hanoi photo walk works

- Nhóm: Offer; Keyword: PHOTO (Route chụp + giá).
- Trạng thái: Draft, chờ Rocky duyệt. Áp dụng cho Instagram + Facebook Page, đăng bằng tài khoản Page.

## C1 — Câu hỏi mồi (trong 5 phút đầu, ghim)
`Which vibe would you pick: Old Quarter streets, lake views or night lights?`

*Dự phòng:* `Day shoot or night shoot? Pick one.`

## C2 — Mẹo thêm + keyword (20–40 phút sau)
`Tip not in the post: tell us what you will wear and we will match the backdrop. Comment PHOTO for routes and prices.`

*Dự phòng:* `One more: mornings are quieter for first-timers who feel shy. Comment PHOTO and we will suggest a time.`
