A Christmas Photoshoot in Hanoi: Outfits, Timing and How a Candid Session Works

December is Hanoi's most photogenic month: soft light, cool air for layered outfits and decorations on every corner. When to book, where to walk, what to wear and how to give a photo walk as a gift.

December is the month Hanoi looks its best on camera. The sun sits low all day, thin cloud softens every face, and the streets are full of lights. It is also cool enough to wear a coat, a scarf and colors that would melt in July.

If you are in Hanoi for the holidays and want photos of yourself, not just of the decorations, this guide covers when to book, where to walk, what to wear and how a candid session works. It also explains how to give a photo walk as a Christmas present.

What a Christmas photoshoot in Hanoi looks like

Forget the studio backdrop with fake snow. A TravelFrame session is a walk. Your photographer-guide plans a route around the light, takes you through the Old Quarter's festive streets and photographs you between the moments: crossing the road, laughing at a Santa with a saxophone, warming your hands on a coffee.

  • Private: just you, your partner, family or friends.
  • Candid: light direction when you want it, natural moments the rest of the time.
  • Local: your photographer doubles as a guide, so you learn what you are looking at.

When to book

The best dates

  • December 1–22: the sweet spot. Decorations are up, and the streets are busy but workable.
  • December 23–24: beautiful, but the cathedral and Hàng Mã get very crowded after dark. We plan these sessions to finish before about 5:30pm.
  • December 25: a normal working day in Vietnam, so mornings are calm. A good choice for families.
  • December 26–31: Christmas decorations mix with New Year lights. Great for "end of the year" sessions.

The best time of day

  • 3:30–5pm: golden hour. The sun sets around 5:20pm in December.
  • 5:20–6pm: blue hour. The sky still has color while the Christmas lights come on. This is the window for glowing backgrounds.
  • Early morning: quiet streets and soft light, but most decorations are switched off.

The weather is usually dry. If it rains, we move to covered streets and cafés or reschedule. See Hanoi weather in December.

Three festive routes

1. Old Quarter Christmas loop (2 hours)

Hàng Mã's decoration shops in the late-afternoon light, a short walk to St. Joseph's Cathedral and its tree, then Hoàn Kiếm Lake as the lights come on. The most "Christmas" of the three. Details in our Hàng Mã photo walk.

2. French Quarter elegance (2 hours)

Tràng Tiền Plaza's lit facade, yellow colonial villas, the Opera House and wide tree-lined streets. Best with a long coat or a dress. See our French Quarter guide.

3. Cozy café and back lanes (1–2 hours)

Steam from a phở pot, an egg coffee in a decorated café and quiet alleys. Ideal if you want warm, intimate photos rather than big landmarks.

What to wear for a Christmas photoshoot in Hanoi

December afternoons are around 20°C and evenings cooler, so you can layer without overheating.

  • Colors: red, deep green, cream, camel and burgundy. They stand out against yellow walls and grey skies and look festive without a Santa hat.
  • Textures: knitwear, wool coats and corduroy photograph beautifully in soft light.
  • Couples: coordinate, don't match. One neutral and one color usually works best.
  • Accessories: a scarf, a beret or a small vintage bag adds movement and warmth.
  • Shoes: comfortable ones. You'll walk 2–4 km.
  • Avoid: big logos and neon colors, which compete with the lights.
  • Bring: a warm layer to wear between shots and a lip balm. Dry winter air is unkind to lips.

Give a Hanoi photo walk as a Christmas gift

Know someone heading to Hanoi? A photo walk is a gift they will actually use, and it doesn't need wrapping.

  • You choose the package and the number of hours.
  • We send a gift note you can forward or print, with what's included and how to book.
  • They choose the date once they know their travel plans.

Validity, date changes and payment are confirmed in writing before you pay. Send PHOTO on Instagram and mention "gift."

Frequently asked questions

Can we do a photoshoot on Christmas Eve?

Yes, in the afternoon. After about 6pm on December 24 the cathedral and Hàng Mã are too crowded for relaxed photos, so we plan Christmas Eve sessions to end around sunset.

How long should a Christmas photoshoot be?

One to two hours covers one route. Two to three hours lets you combine the Old Quarter with the lake or the French Quarter at an easy pace.

Do you photograph families and couples?

Yes. Sessions are private, for solo travelers, couples, friends and families. For couple ideas, see our couple and honeymoon photoshoot guide.

Can you include Train Street?

No. We don't include the residential railway corridor. Access changes, and the railway safety zone is enforced. We photograph Hanoi's railway heritage from public areas instead.

Can I buy a photo walk as a gift?

Yes. Choose the package and hours, and we send a gift note. The recipient books the date later. Terms are confirmed in writing before payment.
