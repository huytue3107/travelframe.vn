Hanoi Weather in December: Real Temperatures, What to Wear and the Best Photo Light

December in Hanoi is cool, dry and very walkable, with average highs near 22°C and damp nights that feel colder. What to expect, what to pack and when to take photos.

December is one of the easiest months to walk Hanoi. The heat and storms of summer are gone, the sky is often pale and soft, and the city smells of roasting chestnuts and charcoal grills.

It is also the start of winter. Nights get chilly, the air is damp and many old buildings and cafés have no heating. Pack for both.

Below is what the numbers say, what it actually feels like and what to wear. Data last checked November 12, 2026.

What December actually feels like

Early December often still feels like late autumn. Afternoons can be warm enough for a T-shirt, and evenings need a light jacket.

Mid to late December is when winter shows up. Northeast monsoon winds bring cool, grey days. When a cold front arrives, the temperature can drop 5–8°C in a day and stay low for a week.

The cold in Hanoi is a damp cold. At 15°C with 75% humidity, it can feel colder than the number suggests, especially indoors. Many homes, guesthouses and street cafés are open to the air. Hotels usually have reverse-cycle air conditioning that can heat the room. Ask before you book if this matters to you.

On Christmas Eve 2025, the evening temperature in central Hanoi was about 15°C, and a cold front brought rain later that night. Crowds still filled the cathedral square.

Does it rain or snow in Hanoi in December?

It rarely rains hard. December is one of Hanoi's driest months. Expect a few days of fine drizzle rather than storms. A small umbrella or a light rain shell is enough.

It does not snow in Hanoi. Snow and frost are possible on the highest mountains in the far north, such as Fansipan near Sapa, but not in the city.

Air quality: check it in the morning

Winter is also when Hanoi's air quality is often at its worst. Cool, still air can trap haze over the city, especially in the early morning and at night.

  • Check an air quality app before long outdoor walks.
  • Pack a few well-fitting masks if you are sensitive to smog.
  • Plan longer walks for late morning and afternoon, when the haze often lifts.

What to wear in Hanoi in December

Think layers, not a heavy coat. You'll go from a cool morning to a mild afternoon and back to a chilly night.

  • Base: T-shirts or light long sleeves.
  • Middle: a sweater, cardigan or fleece.
  • Outer: a light down jacket or a windproof jacket. Locals wear puffer jackets as soon as it drops under 18°C.
  • Bottom: jeans or trousers. Shorts work only on warm afternoons.
  • Feet: closed walking shoes and warm socks. Pavements are uneven.
  • Extras: a scarf, a compact umbrella and a mask.
  • Temples and churches: cover shoulders and knees.

Going on to Sapa or a Hạ Long Bay cruise? Add thermal layers, gloves and a warm hat. Our Vietnam in December guide covers each region.

The best light for photos in December

December light in Hanoi is a gift for photos. The sun sits low all day, and thin cloud acts like a giant softbox.

  • Golden hour: about 3:30–5pm. The sun sets around 5:20pm, so start earlier than you would at home.
  • Blue hour: about 5:20–6pm, when the Christmas lights come on and the sky still has color.
  • Overcast days: perfect for portraits. Skin looks even and nobody squints.
  • Colors that work: red, mustard, cream and camel stand out against Hanoi's yellow walls and grey winter skies.

Warm-up food for cold days

Winter is the best season for Hanoi's hot food. A bowl of phở at 7am, a hot pot in the evening and an egg coffee in between will keep you going. Our Old Quarter food guide lists 12 spots with addresses and hours.

Is December a good time to visit Hanoi?

Yes. It is dry, cool and comfortable for walking, and the city is decorated for Christmas and New Year. The trade-offs are busy hotels around Christmas and New Year, chilly nights and some hazy mornings.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hanoi cold in December?

It is cool, not freezing. Daytime highs average around 20–22°C and nights around 14–16°C. During cold snaps, nights can drop below 10°C, and the damp air makes it feel colder.

Does it rain a lot in Hanoi in December?

No. December usually gets only 20–30 mm of rain across the whole month, mostly as light drizzle.

Does it snow in Hanoi?

No. Snow is rare even in Vietnam's high mountains and does not happen in Hanoi.

What should I pack for Hanoi in December?

Layers: T-shirts, a sweater or fleece, a light down or windproof jacket, trousers, closed shoes, a scarf, a small umbrella and a few masks for hazy days.

Is late December colder than early December?

Usually, yes. Temperatures keep falling through the month. The coldest stretch of the year is normally in January.
