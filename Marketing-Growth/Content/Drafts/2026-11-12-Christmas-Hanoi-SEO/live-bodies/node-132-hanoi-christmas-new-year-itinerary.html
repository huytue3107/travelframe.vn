5 Days in Hanoi Over Christmas and New Year: A Slow Itinerary (with a Phat Diem Day Trip)

A relaxed five-day plan for Christmas in Hanoi: decorated streets at blue hour, Christmas Eve at the cathedral, a day trip to the stone cathedral of Phat Diem and a variation if you're staying for New Year.

Christmas in Hanoi is busy, festive and cold enough for a scarf. It is also easy to overdo. This plan keeps the days slow: one main thing each morning, one each afternoon and the evenings for lights and food.

It runs from December 23 to 27, 2026, so you are in the city for Christmas Eve (Thursday) and Christmas Day (Friday). A New Year variation is at the end. Base yourself in or near the Old Quarter so you can walk most of it.

Day 1 (Wed, Dec 23): arrive and ease in

  • Arrival: our airport to Old Quarter guide covers taxis, Grab and the bus.
  • Afternoon: a loose walk around the Old Quarter. Get your bearings with our Old Quarter guide.
  • Blue hour (about 5:20–6pm): Hàng Mã Street as the Christmas shops light up. It's busy but not yet at Christmas Eve levels. See the Hàng Mã photo walk.
  • Dinner: something hot and simple, like phở or bún chả. See where to eat in the Old Quarter.

Day 2 (Thu, Dec 24): Christmas Eve

  • Morning: the Temple of Literature, founded in 1070 and later home to Vietnam's first national university. Go early, before school groups.
  • Lunch: long and lazy. You'll be up late.
  • 3:30–5pm: St. Joseph's Cathedral and its Christmas tree in the soft late light, before the crowds. This is the best window for photos, so it's also the slot we recommend for a Christmas photoshoot.
  • Early dinner: book ahead if you want a Christmas menu. Ideas in Christmas dinner in Hanoi.
  • Night: join the crowds at the cathedral and the lake, or attend a vigil Mass. Details in our St. Joseph's Cathedral guide.

Day 3 (Fri, Dec 25): Phát Diệm Cathedral and Tràng An

Christmas Day is a normal working day in Vietnam, so it's a good day to leave the city. Phát Diệm lies further south than the usual Ninh Bình stops, so a car with a driver is the easiest way to combine it with Tràng An.

Phát Diệm Stone Cathedral

About 120 km from Hanoi, Phát Diệm is one of the most unusual churches in Asia. Father Trần Lục built the complex between 1875 and 1899 from stone and ironwood, in the shape of a Vietnamese temple: curved tiled roofs, a stone gatehouse and wooden pillars inside. The main cathedral was completed in 1891.

At Christmas it is a major pilgrimage site for northern Catholics. Expect decorations, Masses and local crowds. Dress modestly and keep quiet inside.

Tràng An

About 30 km from Phát Diệm (roughly 50 minutes by car), Tràng An's rowing-boat trips weave through limestone karsts and caves. Bring a warm layer: it's cool on the water in December.

  • Timing: leave Hanoi by 7:30am, Phát Diệm late morning, lunch, Tràng An in the afternoon, back by about 7pm.
  • Evening: Christmas night is a Friday, so the walking zone around Hoàn Kiếm Lake should be open from around 7pm.

Day 4 (Sat, Dec 26): the French Quarter

  • Morning: walk the French Quarter: the Opera House, Tràng Tiền Street and the old colonial villas. Our French Quarter guide has a route.
  • Afternoon: a museum and a warm coffee. Hỏa Lò Prison and the Vietnamese Women's Museum are both close by.
  • Evening: the weekend Old Quarter night market, which runs Friday to Sunday evenings.

Day 5 (Sun, Dec 27): West Lake and Long Biên Bridge

  • Morning: Trấn Quốc Pagoda and a walk or bike ride along West Lake.
  • Late afternoon: Long Biên Bridge for the sunset over the Red River. Walk the pedestrian path on the side, never on the tracks.
  • Farewell dinner: chả cá or a shared hot pot.

Staying for New Year instead?

Shift the plan to December 27–31 and swap Day 5 for New Year's Eve. December 31, 2026 is a Thursday, and January 1 is a Friday public holiday. Have an early dinner, then head to Hoàn Kiếm Lake for the countdown. See our New Year's Eve in Hanoi guide.

Tips for the holiday week

  • Book early: hotels near the lake and Christmas dinners fill up.
  • Pack layers: nights can drop to around 10–15°C. See Hanoi weather in December.
  • Walk at night: taxis can't get near the cathedral or the lake on busy evenings.
  • Keep one slot free: the best Hanoi moments are often a long coffee you didn't plan.

Frequently asked questions

How many days do I need in Hanoi at Christmas?

Three days covers Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and the main sights. Five days lets you add a day trip and slow down.

Is Phát Diệm worth visiting at Christmas?

Yes, if you are interested in architecture or Catholic Vietnam. It is busier than usual, but the decorations and the atmosphere are special. Visit outside Mass times if you want to look around inside.

Can I visit Phát Diệm by public transport?

Yes, but it's slow. Take a bus or train to Ninh Bình, then a taxi for the last 30 km or so. A car with driver is much easier for a day trip.
