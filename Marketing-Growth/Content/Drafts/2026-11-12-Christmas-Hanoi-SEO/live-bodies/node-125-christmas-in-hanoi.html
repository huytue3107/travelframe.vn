Christmas in Hanoi 2026: Lights, Midnight Mass and What's Actually Open

Christmas isn't a public holiday in Vietnam, but Hanoi turns Christmas Eve into a street festival. Where to go, when the crowds peak and what's open on December 25.

Christmas is not a public holiday in Vietnam. On Friday, December 25, 2026, banks, schools and most offices in Hanoi will be open as usual.

And yet, on the night of December 24, the center of Hanoi fills up like a street festival. Families, students and couples pour into the streets around the cathedral, the lake and the toy-shop street of Hàng Mã. Many of them are not Catholic. They come for the lights, the music and the photos.

This guide covers where to go, when the crowds peak, what the weather does and what's open on Christmas Day. We checked it on November 12, 2026. City events for 2026 are not announced yet, so we will update this page in December.

Christmas in Hanoi: quick answers

  • Is Christmas a holiday in Vietnam? No. December 25 is a normal working day. The official holiday is New Year's Day, January 1.
  • When is the big night? Christmas Eve, December 24, from about 6pm to past midnight.
  • Where? St. Joseph's Cathedral, Hàng Mã Street, Hoàn Kiếm Lake, Tràng Tiền Plaza and the big malls.
  • How cold is it? December in Hanoi averages about 16–22°C. Nights can drop into the low teens, so bring a warm layer.
  • Is it worth it? Yes, if you like festive streets and don't mind crowds. Hanoi does Christmas with more energy than most visitors expect.

Where to see Christmas in Hanoi

1. St. Joseph's Cathedral (Nhà Thờ Lớn)

This is the heart of Christmas in Hanoi. The neo-Gothic cathedral at 40 Nhà Chung was completed for Christmas 1886. Each December the square in front gets a giant tree, a nativity scene and colored lights on the towers.

On Christmas Eve, Hanoi churches hold vigil Masses from around 6pm until midnight. The main celebration is usually around 9–10pm. At midnight the cathedral bells ring in Christmas Day, and the square is packed shoulder to shoulder.

Want the full visiting guide, with the best light and where to stand? Read our St. Joseph's Cathedral guide.

2. Hàng Mã Street

Hàng Mã is Hanoi's festival-supply street. It does lanterns for Mid-Autumn, masks for Halloween and, in December, a solid wall of tinsel, trees, Santa suits and snowmen. It is the most photogenic street in the Old Quarter this month.

Go after dark for the full glow, or late afternoon for softer light and fewer people. Some shops now charge a small fee for photos inside. If you plan to shoot, buy something small first. Our Hàng Mã Christmas photo guide has the details.

3. Hoàn Kiếm Lake and the walking zone

The streets around the lake close to traffic from Friday evening to Sunday night, usually from about 7pm. On December 25, 2026, that means Christmas night falls on a walking-zone Friday. Expect street performers, families and lit trees along the shore.

Christmas Eve is a Thursday, so the walking zone may not be open. Traffic around the lake is heavy, so walk rather than ride.

4. Tràng Tiền Plaza and the French Quarter

The department store at the south end of the lake wraps its whole facade in lights each December. From there, walk down Tràng Tiền Street to the Opera House. Our French Quarter guide covers the route.

5. Quieter churches: Cửa Bắc and Hàm Long

If the cathedral crowd is too much, try Cửa Bắc Church at 56 Phan Đình Phùng or Hàm Long Church at 21 Hàm Long. Both decorate for Christmas and feel calmer. Cửa Bắc has listed a Sunday Mass in English in recent years. Check the parish notice board for 2026 times.

6. The big malls

For families, Hanoi's malls put up the biggest trees. In 2025, Lotte Mall Tây Hồ, Lotte Center, Vincom Bà Triệu and Aeon Mall Long Biên were among the most visited. Displays change every year.

A simple Christmas Eve plan

  1. 3:30–5pm: Photos at the cathedral while the light is soft and the square is still calm.
  2. 5–6:30pm: Walk to Hàng Mã as the shop lights come on.
  3. 6:30–8pm: Early dinner. Book ahead for anywhere with a Christmas menu. See Christmas dinner in Hanoi.
  4. 8pm onward: Back to the cathedral or the lake for the atmosphere. If you want to attend Mass, arrive at least an hour early.

In past years, police closed the street in front of the cathedral to vehicles from late afternoon on December 24. Plan to arrive on foot.

What's open on Christmas Day?

Almost everything. December 25 is a regular Friday in Vietnam. Restaurants, cafés, shops, markets and most sights keep normal hours. Embassies and international schools may close.

That makes Christmas Day a good day for sightseeing. Many visitors are still recovering from Christmas Eve, and the morning streets are calm.

Practical tips

  • Dress in layers. Late December nights can feel cold, especially after rain. Our Hanoi weather in December guide has a packing list.
  • Book a Grab pickup a block away. Drivers can't reach the cathedral or the lake on busy nights.
  • Keep your phone in a front pocket. Crowds are friendly, but they are dense.
  • Carry small cash. Street snacks, coffee and Hàng Mã souvenirs are cash-first.
  • Be respectful at Mass. Cover shoulders and knees, stay quiet and don't use flash inside.

Frequently asked questions

Is Christmas a public holiday in Vietnam?

No. December 25 is a normal working day. Vietnam's official holidays at this time of year are New Year's Day on January 1 and Tết, the Lunar New Year, which falls in February 2027.

Do people celebrate Christmas in Hanoi?

Yes. Catholics are around 7% of Vietnam's population, and they celebrate with Mass and family meals. Many non-Catholic Hanoians also go out on Christmas Eve to see the lights and take photos. For the background, read how Vietnam celebrates Christmas.

Where is midnight Mass in Hanoi?

St. Joseph's Cathedral is the main venue. Evening Masses on December 24 usually run from about 6pm, with the main celebration around 9–10pm and bells at midnight. Exact 2026 times are posted by the parish in December.

Is Hanoi cold at Christmas?

Cool rather than cold. Daytime highs are often around 20°C. Nights can drop to 10–15°C, and the damp air makes it feel colder.

What should I do in Hanoi on Christmas Day?

Sightsee in the calm morning, have a long lunch, then join the Friday-evening walking zone around Hoàn Kiếm Lake. If you are staying through New Year, see our Christmas and New Year itinerary.
