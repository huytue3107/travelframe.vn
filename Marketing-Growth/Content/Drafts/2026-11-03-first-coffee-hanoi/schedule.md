Ordering your first coffee in Hanoi can feel like a guessing game. ☕

Here is a quick guide so you get exactly what you want:

1. Hot or iced: Say “nóng” for hot, or “đá” for iced.
2. Milk choice: In Vietnam, milk coffee (“cà phê sữa”) means sweet condensed milk by default. If you prefer fresh milk, ask for “sữa tươi”.
3. Sweetness: Vietnamese coffee is naturally bold and sweet. Ask for “ít ngọt” (less sweet) before the barista starts brewing.
4. Not sure? Just point to the menu and ask what is inside. People are happy to help.

Pro move: Vietnamese coffee uses Robusta beans, which carry about twice the caffeine of Arabica. Take your time with those first few sips.
