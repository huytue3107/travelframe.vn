## Part 1: Frequently Asked Questions About Hanoi

### 1. Is Hanoi safe for solo female travelers?
**Answer:**  
Yes, Hanoi is considered one of the safest capital cities in Southeast Asia for solo travelers. Violent crime is exceptionally rare, and locals are welcoming and respectful. Standard travel precautions apply: watch your bag near busy scooter traffic, keep valuables in front of you at night markets, and always use official ride-hailing apps like Grab for airport transfers.

### 2. What is the best time of year to visit Hanoi?
**Answer:**  
Autumn (September through November) is widely regarded as the most pleasant season, featuring clear blue skies, comfortable temperatures (22°C–28°C / 71°F–82°F), and minimal rainfall. Spring (March to April) is also pleasant with blooming flowers, while winter (December to February) brings cool, atmospheric weather perfect for hot noodle soups.

### 3. How many days should I spend in Hanoi?
**Answer:**  
We recommend at least **3 full days** to explore the historic Old Quarter, discover quiet French Quarter heritage boulevards, savor authentic street cuisine, and visit major cultural sites like the Temple of Literature. Many travelers use Hanoi as their 3-to-4 day base before taking excursions to Halong Bay or Ninh Binh.

### 4. Do I need a visa to visit Vietnam in 2026?
**Answer:**  
Most international visitors require a visa or an e-visa. As of 2026, Vietnam offers a 90-day multiple-entry e-visa for citizens of all nationalities via the official government immigration portal (`evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn`). We advise applying at least 7 to 10 days before your departure.

### 5. What currency is used, and can I use credit cards?
**Answer:**  
The currency is the Vietnamese Dong (VND). While hotels, upscale restaurants, and convenience stores accept international credit cards (Visa/Mastercard), street food stalls, local cafes, and traditional market vendors operate on cash only. ATMs from international and major local banks are widely available throughout the Old Quarter.

## Part 2: Frequently Asked Questions About TravelFrame Photo Tours

### 6. What exactly is TravelFrame?
**Answer:**  
TravelFrame is a private walking photo experience in Hanoi. Rather than a rigid, crowded tour bus or a stiff studio portrait shoot, you spend 1.5 to 2.5 hours exploring hidden corners, ancient courtyards, and vibrant street life with a bilingual local photographer who acts as your local friend and storyteller—capturing natural, candid, magazine-quality memories as you walk.

### 7. What is included in every TravelFrame session?
**Answer:**  
Every private session includes:
- A dedicated, English-speaking local documentary photographer.
- A personalized walking route through secret Old Quarter alleys and scenic spots.
- Local coffee or tea stop at a historic heritage cafe.
- Cultural storytelling and local insider recommendations.
- Professionally color-graded, high-resolution digital photos delivered in a private online gallery.

### 8. How many photos will I receive, and how fast are they delivered?
**Answer:**  
Depending on your package (Solo or Couple/Group), you will receive between **30 and 60+ curated, professionally edited, high-resolution digital images**, plus full rights for personal download and printing. We know you want to share your travel memories while you're still on your trip, so we guarantee delivery within **24 hours** via a private online cloud gallery link.

### 9. What if I don't know how to pose?
**Answer:**  
That is our specialty! 95% of our guests say they feel awkward in front of cameras. We never ask you to hold stiff, unnatural poses. We guide you through quiet streets, chat about local life, point out interesting architecture, and capture candid, genuine laughs and unposed expressions while you are simply enjoying the experience.

### 10. What happens if it rains on our tour date?
**Answer:**  
A light Hanoi drizzle actually creates incredible atmospheric photos with glossy reflections on ancient stone pavements and vibrant umbrella colors. However, if there is severe heavy downpour or storms, we will gladly reschedule your session to another time slot or another day during your stay at no extra charge, or offer an indoor heritage cafe & temple itinerary.

### 11. Can I bring an outfit change?
**Answer:**  
Yes! For our standard 2-hour and 2.5-hour sessions, you are welcome to bring a second outfit (for example: comfortable casual travel wear for the street walk, and a traditional *áo dài* or elegant dress for temple courtyards). We know comfortable heritage cafes along our route where you can easily change.

### 12. How do I book a session?
**Answer:**  
You can reserve your session directly on our website at [travelframe.vn](https://travelframe.vn/) or send us a direct message on Instagram (`@travelframe.vn`). Because our sessions are strictly private and slots are limited per photographer each day, we recommend reserving at least 3 to 7 days in advance.
