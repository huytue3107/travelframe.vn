Five Vietnamese phrases that make Hanoi easier. Your Hanoi auntie is listening. 👀

Cảm ơn: thank you (gahm uhn)
Bao nhiêu: how much? (bow nyew)
Ít ngọt: less sweet
Không cay: not spicy (khom kai)
Tính tiền: the bill, please (ting tee-en)

Pronunciation guides are approximate. Locals love the effort more than perfect tones.
