# 5 Vietnamese phrases that make Hanoi easier

## Hook (0-3s)

Five Vietnamese phrases. Your Hanoi auntie will check your pronunciation.

## Beats

1. Cảm ơn: thank you. Say it like gahm uhn. Use it everywhere.

2. Bao nhiêu: how much? Bow nyew. Point, then ask.

3. Ít ngọt: less sweet. Your coffee will thank you.

4. Không cay: not spicy. Khom kai. Unless you are brave.

5. Tính tiền: the bill, please. Ting tee-en. Auntie approves.
