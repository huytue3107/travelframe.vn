## Where we shoot

Every route is planned around the light, your pace and current local conditions. Popular backdrops include:

- **Old Quarter lanes** — weathered shopfronts, craft streets, tube houses and hidden cafés.
- **Hoàn Kiếm Lake & the Huc Bridge** — best in soft morning light.
- **French Quarter & the Opera House** — colonial facades and wide boulevards.
- **Long Biên Bridge & Phùng Hưng railway arches** — Hanoi's railway heritage, photographed from public areas.
- **St. Joseph's Cathedral, Temple of Literature, West Lake** — on longer walks.

> Note on Train Street: we do not include or guarantee access to the residential railway corridor. Access changes and authorities enforce the railway safety zone. Solid, contrasting colors photograph best against Hanoi's yellow walls and green shutters. Bring comfortable shoes, sunglasses or a vintage accessory, and a relaxed mindset. Respectful dress (shoulders and knees covered) is needed for temples.

## Frequently asked questions

**I'm awkward in front of the camera. Will this work for me?**
Yes. We photograph you while you explore, so most shots are natural moments rather than poses. We give light direction only when you want it.

**How long should my photoshoot be?**
Two hours covers one area of the Old Quarter. Three to four hours lets us combine the Old Quarter with the lake or French Quarter at a relaxed pace.

**When will I get my photos?**
Phone package: within 1 hour. Explorer: 10 edited photos within 2 days. Complete and VIP: retouched photos within 3 days.

**What time of day is best?**
Early morning (soft light, fewer crowds) or late afternoon golden hour. We suggest a time once we know your dates and the forecast.

**Can you take us to Train Street?**
We don't include or guarantee the residential railway corridor. We can photograph Hanoi's railway heritage from public areas such as the Phùng Hưng arches or Long Biên Bridge.

**What if it rains?**
We adjust the route toward covered streets, cafés and arcades, or reschedule. Confirm the rain policy in writing when you book.
