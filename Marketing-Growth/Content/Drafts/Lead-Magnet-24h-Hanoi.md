## Welcome

You've got 24 hours. That's enough.

Hanoi rewards the curious — but only if you know where to look. This guide
is built from hundreds of hours walking these streets, not from a travel
blog roundup.

Every spot below is walkable or a short Grab ride apart. No rushing. No

## The Day

### 5:30 AM — Sunrise at Hoan Kiem Lake

The lake is at its best before the city wakes up.

- Walk the full loop (30 min, ~1.7 km).
- Ngoc Son Temple opens at 7 AM (30,000 VND entry).
- Watch the tai chi groups on the south side.
- Pro move: Bring a light layer. Mornings can be cool, even in summer.

### 7:00 AM — Breakfast: Phở or Bánh Cuốn

Two options:

- **Phở Thìn** (13 Lo Duc) — beef pho with stir-fried beef. Bold, smoky.
  Expect a queue. [VERIFY price before publish — estimated 60,000-80,000 VND]
- **Bánh Cuốn Bà Hoành** (66 To Hien Thanh) — steamed rice rolls. Lighter
  start. [VERIFY still operating + price before publish]

Pro move: Point at what the person next to you ordered. It's usually good.

### 8:30 AM — Old Quarter Walk

Start from Dong Xuan Market, head south through Hang Ma (paper goods
street) → Hang Bac (silver street) → Hang Gai (silk street).

- Don't buy at the first shop. Walk the whole street first.
- The real texture is above eye level — look up at the balconies.
- Ask before photographing people, especially vendors.

### 10:30 AM — Egg Coffee Break

- **Cafe Giang** (39 Nguyen Huu Huan) — the original since 1946.
- Order: Cà phê trứng nóng (hot egg coffee).
- Sit upstairs for the view down.

[VERIFY: price range before publish — estimated 35,000-45,000 VND]

### 11:30 AM — Temple of Literature

Vietnam's first university, built in 1070.

- Entry: 30,000 VND (checked 2026-09-06).
- Allow 45-60 minutes.
- Best photos: the stone stele courtyard (3rd courtyard).
- Pro move: Visit on a weekday morning to avoid school groups.

### 1:00 PM — Lunch: Bún Chả

The dish Obama ate.

- **Bún Chả Hương Liên** (24 Le Van Huu) — "Obama Bun Cha."
  [VERIFY still operating + price — estimated 50,000-70,000 VND]

Or explore: walk into any place that's packed with locals at noon. If locals
are eating there, the food is good.

### 2:30 PM — Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum Area

- Mausoleum: free, open Tue-Thu + Sat-Sun mornings. Closed Mon + Fri.
  [VERIFY current schedule before publish]
- One Pillar Pagoda: 5 minutes walk, free.
- Presidential Palace gardens: peaceful walk.

Pro move: Dress modestly (covered shoulders and knees) for the mausoleum.

### 4:00 PM — West Lake Afternoon

Grab to Truc Bach / West Lake area (~15 min from center).

- Walk the causeway between Truc Bach and West Lake.
- **Quan Thanh Temple:** one of Hanoi's four guardian temples.
  Entry: 10,000 VND.
- Sit at a lakeside café and watch the late afternoon light.

### 6:00 PM — Sunset + Street Life

Head back to the Old Quarter.

- If it's a weekend (Fri-Sun evening): the walking streets around Hoan Kiem
  open up. Live music, local families, street performers.
  [VERIFY: current walking street schedule before publish]
- If weekday: walk along Hoan Kiem's east side. The light on the water is
  worth the walk.

### 7:00 PM — Dinner: Choose Your Style

- **Street food option:** Ta Hien street — beer corner + grilled skewers.
  Budget: ~100,000-150,000 VND for a full meal with drinks.
- **Sit-down option:** Chả Cá Lã Vọng (14 Cha Ca) — turmeric fish.
  [VERIFY price before publish — estimated 150,000-250,000 VND]

### 8:30 PM — Night Walk

Hanoi at night is a different city.

- The Old Quarter lit up.
- Long Bien Bridge: walk halfway for the view (go with a friend,
  watch your belongings).
- Night market (Fri-Sun): Hang Dao to Dong Xuan.

### 10:00 PM — Final Stop

End the day with:
- Kem Tràng Tiền (ice cream) near the lake.
- Or a quiet Bia Hơi (draft beer, ~10,000 VND) at a street corner.

## Practical Tips

1. **Get Grab.** Download before you arrive. Works for cars and motorbike
   taxis. Always book in-app, check the plate.
2. **Carry small bills.** Many street food places don't have change for
   500,000 VND notes.
3. **Exchange at banks.** Never on the street.
4. **Crossing the road:** Walk steady, don't stop suddenly. Traffic flows
   around you.
5. **Water:** Buy bottled (5,000-10,000 VND). Tap water is not safe to
   drink.
