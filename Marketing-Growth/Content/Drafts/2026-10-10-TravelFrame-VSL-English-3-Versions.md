# TravelFrame VSL — Three English Versions

Three English scripts compiled from the versions developed in this chat. Proposed CTA: message TravelFrame on Instagram.

## 1. Feel like a model on your travels

Ever wanted to feel like a model on your travels?

Someone finds the light, chooses the angle, and gently guides you through each shot. You get to relax and enjoy being in the frame.

With TravelFrame, it starts with a walk through Hanoi.

We’ll take you to local corners that suit your style and help you find natural ways to stand, move, and look into the camera.

You don’t need any posing experience. We might start with a slow walk, a glance over your shoulder, or a smile in the middle of a conversation.

Together, we’ll create photos that feel personal to you and this trip.

Message TravelFrame with your Hanoi dates and the kind of photos you love. Let’s plan your photo walk together.

## 2. Get comfortable in front of the camera

Ever found a beautiful spot, then suddenly forgotten what to do with your hands?

You want lovely travel photos, but looking into the camera feels a little awkward.

We’ll ease you into it.

We’ll walk through Hanoi together, chat along the way, and find places that suit you. When you need a little guidance, we’ll help you relax your shoulders, turn toward the light, or take a few slower steps.

You’ll have time to get comfortable, look through the photos, and tell us what you like.

As we go, the photo session becomes part of the fun. You explore the city while we capture your expressions and the moments along the way.

Message TravelFrame with your travel dates and how many people are joining you. We’ll suggest a photo walk that fits your trip.

## 3. Photos that bring you back to the trip

When this trip is over, which moments will you want to keep?

Your first walk down a little street in Hanoi. An afternoon watching the city from a café. The smile on your face as you discover somewhere new.

TravelFrame would love to help you capture those moments.

We’ll explore local corners together, find settings that suit you, and offer gentle posing guidance. While you enjoy the walk, we’ll also look for the spontaneous moments between shots.

A glance over your shoulder. A conversation that makes you laugh. Little details that make these photos feel like your trip.

So when you’re home and looking through them, you can remember how you felt on that street.

Message TravelFrame with what you’d love to remember about Hanoi. Let’s plan your own story in photos.
