### Instagram Reel caption

```text
5 situations that catch first-timers in Hanoi, and how to handle each one:

1. Shoe repair on the street. Someone points at your sneaker, applies glue without asking, and asks for 300,000 VND. Say a firm "no thank you" with a wave of your hand before they start.
2. Fruit basket props. Women carrying bamboo shoulder baskets offer to let you wear the hat for a photo, then demand you buy fruit at ten times the regular price. Smile and say "Khong, cam on" (no, thank you) before touching the basket.
3. Metered taxi confusion. Fake taxis mimic reputable brands like Mai Linh or Taxi Group with slightly misspelled logos. Book through the Grab app so you know the price before getting in.
4. Currency confusion. The blue 20,000 VND polymer note looks similar to the cyan 500,000 VND note in low light. Check the zeros before handing cash over.
5. Unpriced street menus. Most stalls post a price board on the wall. If there is no printed menu, ask "bao nhieu mot bat?" (how much for a bowl?) before sitting down.

Save this for your trip to the Old Quarter.
```

## Day 2: Hanoi Train Street

Format: Instagram Reel, Threads post, Facebook post, YouTube Shorts (cross-post Day 1)  
Hook text on screen: What you need to know about Hanoi Train Street  

### Instagram Reel caption

```text
A few things to keep in mind about Hanoi Train Street before you go:

The railway runs through a working residential neighborhood, not an open public park. Because trains run close to doorsteps, local authorities periodically restrict casual walking on the tracks for safety reasons.

If you want to watch the train pass safely:
- Walk to the cafe checkpoints on Tran Phu or Phung Hung street.
- Local cafe owners can seat you behind the safety barriers on their ground floor or upper balcony.
- Buy a drink (usually 35,000 to 50,000 VND for an iced coffee or tea) and stay seated while the train passes.
- Keep your phone and camera inside the barrier line.

Check current train schedules with your hotel or cafe, since weekend and weekday timings differ.

#hanoi #trainstreet #hanoitrainstreet #vietnamtravel #oldquarterhanoi
```

### Threads post

```text
The train comes fast and takes up almost the whole street. Respect the cafe owners when they tell you to step back.
```

## Day 5: 5 mistakes first-timers make in Vietnam

Format: Instagram Reel, Threads post, Facebook post  
Hook text on screen: 5 mistakes travelers make on their first Vietnam trip  

### Instagram Reel caption

```text
5 things travelers often get wrong on their first trip to Vietnam:

1. Packing too many cities into one week. Trying to do Hanoi, Halong, Hoi An, and Saigon in 7 days means spending half your holiday in airports. Pick two regions and take your time.
2. Skipping the street food stalls. The best meals in northern Vietnam happen on small plastic stools, not in hotel dining rooms. Look for stalls crowded with locals.
3. Relying entirely on credit cards. Cards work in hotels and large cafes, but street vendors, markets, and local taxis operate strictly on cash.
4. Not downloading Grab before landing. Street hailing outside airports can lead to meter arguments. Grab sets the price up front.
5. Treating Hanoi as a quick transit stop. Give the capital at least 3 full days before heading to the mountains or the coast.

Which one were you close to doing? Let us know in the comments.

#vietnamtravel #hanoitips #vietnamtrip #travelsoutheastasia #backpackingvietnam
```
