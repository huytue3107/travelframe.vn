# Indian Restaurants in Hanoi: Curry, Pure Veg, Jain and South Indian (2026)

Missing dal after a week of noodles? You are not alone.

More Indian travelers now visit Vietnam, and Hanoi's Old Quarter has filled up with Indian kitchens to match. Many are pure vegetarian. Some cook Jain food. A few serve South Indian dosa.

This guide is organized by what you need, not by star ratings. Most places are within a 10-minute walk of each other.

## Where Indian food is in Hanoi

Most Indian restaurants are in **Hoàn Kiếm**, inside or at the edge of the Old Quarter. The streets to know: Hàng Bông, Đường Thành, Hàng Điếu, Lãn Ông and Hàng Buồm. [VERIFY]

There are also a few more upscale Indian restaurants in **West Lake (Tây Hồ)**, where many expats live. They tend to cost more, with a calmer room. [VERIFY]

## North Indian classics

### Namaste Hanoi

- **Address:** 46 Thợ Nhuộm, Hoàn Kiếm
- **Hours:** lunch and dinner, with a break in the afternoon [VERIFY]

One of the longest-running Indian restaurants in the city, and a safe first choice. Big menu, tandoori, curries and a full vegetarian section.

### MAAZI Old Quarter

- **Address:** 57 Đường Thành, Hoàn Kiếm
- **Hours:** late morning to around midnight [VERIFY]

A big, warm dining room on the edge of the Old Quarter. Good for groups who want curry and a cold drink.

## Pure vegetarian and Jain food

This is where Hanoi surprises people. Several Old Quarter kitchens are fully vegetarian.

- **Ram Krishna Vegetarian Indian Cuisine:** pure veg, Jain and vegan, North Indian home-style cooking. Sources list two different addresses, so check the map before you go. [VERIFY]
- **Annam:** a Hindu-run pure-veg kitchen on Hàng Muối. [VERIFY]
- **AMY Pure Veg:** Punjabi and Jain dishes, with Gujarati options. [VERIFY]

**How to order Jain food in Hanoi:** say "Jain" first, then list what to leave out. No onion, no garlic, no root vegetables. Ask whether the kitchen uses the same pans for meat dishes.

**For vegan meals:** ask for no ghee, no cream and no paneer. Most curries can be adapted.

## South Indian: dosa and idli

South Indian food is harder to find than North Indian. **Southern Delights** is one name that focuses on vegetarian South Indian dishes like dosa and idli. [VERIFY address and hours]

If you only want one dosa, call ahead. Smaller kitchens sometimes run out of batter early.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there good Indian food in Hanoi?
Yes. The Old Quarter has a dense cluster of Indian restaurants, from North Indian classics to pure-veg and Jain kitchens.

### Where can I find pure veg Indian food in Hanoi?
In the Old Quarter. Ram Krishna, Annam and AMY are fully vegetarian, and most other Indian restaurants have large veg sections. [VERIFY]

### Can I get Jain food in Hanoi?
Yes. Several Old Quarter restaurants cook Jain dishes on request. Say "Jain" and confirm no onion, garlic or root vegetables.

### Is there South Indian food in Hanoi?
A little. Look for vegetarian South Indian specialists such as Southern Delights, and call ahead for dosa. [VERIFY]

Some are. Ask each restaurant about its meat. See our halal food guide for halal-friendly Indian options.
