# Reel — Pure veg, Jain and curry in Hanoi's Old Quarter

## Production brief
- Format: 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps, 30–34s. Subtitle do pipeline render.
- Voice: nam hoặc nữ, giọng vui, nhịp nhanh hơn reel halal (≈2,5 từ/giây). Nhạc nền tabla nhẹ hoặc lo-fi, không lời.
- Look: màu ấm, cận cảnh món ăn có hơi nóng; 1 cảnh dosa nếu tìm được quán Nam Ấn mở cửa.
- Địa điểm: 2–3 quán Ấn trong Phố cổ (ưu tiên 1 quán thuần chay), 1 cảnh phố Hàng Bông/Đường Thành.

## Hook (0-3s)
Vegetarian, Jain, or just missing dal?
Hanoi's Old Quarter has you covered.

## Beats
**Beat 1 (3-9s) — Where.**
Indian kitchens cluster in a few Old Quarter streets.
Most are a ten-minute walk apart.

**Beat 2 (9-16s) — Pure veg.**
Some are fully vegetarian.
Others have big veg menus next to the tandoori.

**Beat 3 (16-23s) — Jain and vegan.**
Say Jain first.
Then: no onion, no garlic, no root vegetables.
Pro move: for vegan, ask for no ghee, no cream, no paneer.

**Beat 4 (23-28s) — South Indian.**
Dosa is rarer here.
Call ahead, because small kitchens run out of batter.

## CTA
Save this for the night you crave dal.
Send it to the friend who packed instant noodles from home.
