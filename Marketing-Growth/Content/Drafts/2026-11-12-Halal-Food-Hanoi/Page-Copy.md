# Halal Food in Hanoi: Where to Eat Near the Old Quarter (2026)

Hanoi is not Kuala Lumpur. Most street food here uses pork, and many broths start with pork bones.

But you can eat well, and halal, in Hanoi. Most of the options sit in or near the Old Quarter, a short walk from the city's mosque.

## How to read halal labels in Hanoi

Vietnam has no single halal sign that every restaurant uses. So we sort places into three groups:

1. **Certified:** the restaurant shows a halal certificate from a named body. We photograph it.
2. **Muslim-owned:** run by a Muslim family or chef, with halal meat, but no certificate on display.
3. **No pork, by their own word:** a non-Muslim kitchen that says it uses no pork, lard or alcohol in cooking.

What to ask, every time:

- Is the meat halal? Where is it from?
- Do you cook with pork, lard or alcohol (rượu)?
- Is the kitchen separate?

## Visiting the Al-Noor Mosque

- **Address:** 12 Hàng Lược, Hàng Mã, Hoàn Kiếm
- **What it is:** the only mosque in northern Vietnam, built by Indian Muslim traders and in use since around 1890.
- **Friday prayer:** around midday. Check the mosque's official website for current times before you go. [VERIFY current Jumu'ah time]
- **Facilities:** wudu area, and a prayer area for women and children. [VERIFY]

The mosque is small, green and white, and tucked between shops on a busy street. You could walk past it without noticing.

Visitors are welcome, including non-Muslims. Dress modestly, cover shoulders and knees, and remove your shoes. Ask before taking photos, especially during prayer.

Tip: in Hanoi, people say *lợn* for pork. In the south it is *heo*. Both are fine to show on your phone.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it easy to find halal food in Hanoi?
It is harder than in Ho Chi Minh City, but possible. Most halal restaurants are in or near the Old Quarter, within walking distance of the mosque.

### Is pho halal?
Traditional Hanoi beef pho is made with beef, but the meat source and broth are not halal by default. Choose a halal restaurant that serves pho, such as d'LIONS. [VERIFY]

### Where is the mosque in Hanoi?
Al-Noor Mosque is at 12 Hàng Lược in the Old Quarter. It is the only mosque in northern Vietnam.

### What time is Friday prayer at Al-Noor Mosque?
Around midday. Times shift through the year, so check the mosque's official website or Facebook page before you go. [VERIFY]

### Can non-Muslims visit Al-Noor Mosque?
Yes. Dress modestly, remove your shoes and avoid prayer times if you are only visiting.
