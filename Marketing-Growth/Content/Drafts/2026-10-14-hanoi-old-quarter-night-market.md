# Hanoi Old Quarter Night Market (2026): Hours, Route, What to Eat and Tips

*By TravelFrame Editorial Team · Updated October 2026*

Every weekend evening, a long stretch of the Old Quarter closes to motorbikes and turns into a walking market. It is busy, loud and very local. Many families from Hanoi come here too, not just visitors.

This guide covers when it runs, which streets it uses, what to eat and how to enjoy it without getting worn out.

## Quick facts

- **When:** Friday, Saturday and Sunday evenings. *[VERIFY giờ mở/đóng hiện tại]*
- **Where:** from Hàng Đào street, just north of Hoàn Kiếm Lake, toward Đồng Xuân Market.
- **Cost:** free to walk. Bring small notes in Vietnamese đồng for snacks.
- **Time needed:** 1–2 hours at an easy pace.

## The route, street by street

The market starts near the northern edge of Hoàn Kiếm Lake. It runs north along Hàng Đào, then continues through Hàng Ngang and Hàng Đường to the area around Đồng Xuân Market. *[VERIFY tuyến phố năm 2026]*

You do not need a plan. Walk north on one side and come back down the other. Side streets have the quieter food stalls.

**Tip:** Start at the lake end. Then you finish near Đồng Xuân, where street food is easy to find.

## What you will find

- **Clothing and accessories.** T-shirts, bags, sunglasses and phone cases. Quality varies, so check stitching before you pay.
- **Souvenirs.** Small lacquer pieces, magnets, conical hats and printed tote bags.
- **Snacks.** Grilled skewers, fresh fruit, sugarcane juice and sweet soups.
- **Street life.** Families out for a walk, couples, and music on the corners.

## What to eat nearby

The market itself is mostly shopping. For a proper meal, step into the side streets around it:

- **Bún chả:** grilled pork with noodles. See our [bún chả guide](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/bun-cha-chan-local-culinary-guide).
- **Street food basics:** our [honest Hanoi street food guide](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/honest-hanoi-street-food-guide-2026-what-eat-where-sit-and-how-eat-local) explains what to order and how to sit like a local.
- **Late drinks:** [Tạ Hiện street](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/news/ta-hien-street-where-hanoi-nights-come-alive) is a short walk east and stays lively after the market closes.

## The walking street around Hoàn Kiếm Lake

On weekend evenings, the streets around Hoàn Kiếm Lake also close to traffic. *[VERIFY khung giờ phố đi bộ]* You will see street performers, traditional games and families with children. It pairs well with the night market: lake first, then walk north into the market.

## Tips for a better evening

- **Go early in the evening.** It gets more crowded later.
- **Keep your phone in a front pocket.** Crowds make pickpocketing easier.
- **Bargain politely.** Smile, offer a lower price and walk away if it does not feel right. Food prices are usually fixed.
- **Carry cash.** Some stalls take bank transfers through QR codes, but cash is simpler for visitors.
- **Wear comfortable shoes.** You will walk more than you expect.

## Photographing the night market

The light here comes from shop signs, lanterns and stall bulbs. Phones do well if you hold still and tap to expose for the brightest light. Ask before photographing vendors up close. A smile and a gesture toward your camera is usually enough.

Want photos of yourself in the evening lights? A [private Hanoi photoshoot](https://travelframe.sidevn.com/hanoi-photoshoot) can end at the lake just as the walking street opens.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Hanoi night market open every day?
No. It runs on weekend evenings, Friday to Sunday. On weekdays, the same streets are normal shopping streets with traffic.

### Is the night market safe?
It is generally safe. The main risks are crowds and petty theft. Keep valuables close and agree on a meeting point if you get separated.

### How far is it from Hoàn Kiếm Lake?
It starts right at the north end of the lake. You can walk from the lake to Đồng Xuân Market in about 15 minutes without stops.
