# Private Couple Photo Walk in Hanoi

## Listing title

Hanoi: Private Couple Photo Walk with a Local Photographer

Short title (under 60 characters): Hanoi Couple Photo Walk with a Local Photographer

## Summary

A private walk for two through Hanoi's lakeside paths, Old Quarter lanes and lamp-lit cafés. Your photographer is also your local guide, so you get photos of both of you together, plus a slower way to see the city.

## Highlights

- Photos of the two of you together, not one of you holding the phone
- A local photographer-guide who plans the route around the light
- Choose daytime, after dark, or a dressed-up session
- Gentle direction on where to stand and what to do with your hands
- All original photos within 24 hours, edited photos within 48 hours

## Full description

Most couples come home from Hanoi with hundreds of photos and almost none of both of them. This walk fixes that.

We meet, talk about the look you want, and walk at an easy pace. Along the way we stop where the light is good: a lake path in the morning, a quiet lane, a café front once the lamps come on. You walk, talk and hold hands. We tell you the small things, like where to look and when to slow down.

Honeymoon, anniversary, pre-wedding or simply a trip together: tell us what the photos are for, and we plan the route for it.

## What you'll do

Pick one of three styles. Routes change with the light, the weather and what is happening on the street that day.

**Daytime.** Hoàn Kiếm Lake paths, St. Joseph's Cathedral square and Old Quarter lanes such as Tạ Hiện. A coffee stop if you want one.

**After dark.** The lake with the lights on, the cathedral at night and lamp-lit café fronts in the Old Quarter.

**Dressed up.** Suit, gown or your favourite formal outfit against French-era facades and old cafés. Best on Premium or VIP, which cover 2 to 3 areas.

Meeting point: agreed with you after booking, usually near Hoàn Kiếm Lake. [VERIFY]

## Who it's for

- Honeymooners and couples celebrating an anniversary
- Couples who want pre-wedding photos with a Hanoi backdrop
- Couples who feel awkward in front of a camera and want help, not stiff poses

## FAQ

**We are not good at posing. Is that a problem?**
No. Most of the session is walking and talking. We give simple directions as we go.

**Can we bring our own outfits?**
Yes. Bring as many as you like. On VIP an outfit is included.

**When do we get the photos?**
All original photos within 24 hours. Edited photos and video within 48 hours.

**Can we do daytime and after dark in one session?**
Yes, on Premium or VIP. Start an hour or so before sunset and finish once the lamps are on.
