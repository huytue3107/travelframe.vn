## 1. Tại Sao TikTok?

### Vấn đề hiện tại
TravelFrame phụ thuộc 100% vào Instagram cho discovery. Điều này tạo
single-point-of-failure: nếu IG thay đổi thuật toán hoặc giảm reach,
toàn bộ acquisition pipeline bị ảnh hưởng.

### Cơ hội TikTok
- **Reach cao hơn ở giai đoạn đầu:** TikTok ưu tiên nội dung mới hơn
  follower count. Accounts mới có thể nhận 10K-100K views trên video đầu.
- **Persona fit:** Solo female 23-40 (P1) và Creator (P3) rất active
  trên TikTok.
- **Search engine behavior:** TikTok đang thay thế Google cho Gen Z
  travel search. Keywords như "hanoi travel tips" có search volume cao.
- **Cross-pollination:** TikTok followers có thể discover IG account →
  tăng IG growth.

### Rủi ro
- Thêm platform = thêm production workload.
- TikTok audience có thể khác IG (younger, different intent).
- Chưa có TikTok DM automation (ManyChat limited).

## 3. Content Strategy

### Content Mix (mirroring IG success)

```
50% Travel Utility   → Repost IG winners + TikTok-native versions
25% Destination Discovery → Hanoi/Vietnam reveals
15% Photography Bridge    → Photo tips, spots, before/after
10% TravelFrame/Offer     → Tour BTS, experience walkthrough
```

### Cross-Post vs Native

| Approach | Khi nào dùng | Lưu ý |
|---|---|---|
| **Cross-post từ IG Reels** | Mỗi Reel mới | Upload native (không share link), bỏ IG watermark, thay caption cho TikTok keywords |
| **TikTok-native** | 1-2 lần/tuần | Raw POV, ambient sounds, faster cuts, trending audio |

### Format Priorities

1. **POV Walking Tour** — iPhone footage, ambient street sounds, text overlay
2. **"Things I wish I knew"** — talking head or text-on-video
3. **Before/After** — tourist photo vs TravelFrame photo (brand bridge)
4. **Quick Guide** — fast-paced 15-30s tips (clone IG Quick Guide winner)
5. **Day in the Life** — guide preparing for a tour (humanize brand)

### TikTok-Specific Adjustments

| IG Reel Style | TikTok Adaptation |
|---|---|
| Polished, edited | More raw, authentic, "iPhone feel" |
| 30-60s | Can go 15-90s, test both |
| Subtle CTA | Bolder CTA with trending sounds |
| Caption-heavy | On-screen text emphasis (TikTok users mute more) |
| Save/Share CTA | "Follow for Hanoi tips" (follower growth focus) |

## 5. TikTok SEO

TikTok functions as a search engine in 2026. Optimize for:

### Caption Keywords
Include in every caption:
- Destination: `hanoi`, `vietnam`
- Topic: `travel tips`, `street food`, `photo spots`
- Year: `2026`
- Niche: `solo travel`, `photo tour`, `local guide`

### On-Screen Text
- First 1-3 seconds: keyword-rich hook text
- Throughout: key takeaways as text overlays

### Hashtags
Core set (every post):
```
#hanoi #vietnamtravel #hanoitravel #traveltips #travelframe
```

Rotating (match content):
```
#solotravel #solofemaletravel #hanoifood #hanoiphotography
#trainstreet #vietnamtips #travelhacks #hiddenhanoi
```
