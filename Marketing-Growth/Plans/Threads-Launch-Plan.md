| **Organic reach 2026** | Rất cao — 500M+ MAU, still early-mover | Đã bão hòa ở nhiều niche |
| **Phù hợp TravelFrame voice** | ✅ Warm local friend, conversational | ⚠️ Cần adapt sang fast-cut video |
| **Funnel integration** | Profile → IG profile → bio link → website | Profile → bio link (không sync IG) |
| **International audience** | Mạnh: US, EU, India, Brazil, APAC | Mạnh nhưng younger skew |
| **Algorithm signal** | Replies + genuine engagement | Watch time + completion rate |

### Chiến lược tổng thể Giai đoạn 1

```
Instagram Reels → Discovery + Save/Share (visual-first)
         ↕ cross-pollinate
Threads → Community + Trust + Search Discovery (text-first)

    IG Profile → Bio Link → Website → Booking
```

## 3. Content Strategy: "Reply-First, Post-Second"

### Nguyên tắc cốt lõi

Threads algorithm 2026 ưu tiên **genuine engagement** (replies) hơn
broadcasting. Quy tắc:

> **Spend 60% time replying, 40% posting.**

### Content Pillars

| Pillar | % | Mục đích | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Travel Utility** | 40% | Value + Save | Tips, hacks, itinerary advice |
| **Local Perspective** | 25% | Trust + Authority | "As someone who lives in Hanoi..." |
| **Conversation Starters** | 20% | Engagement + Reach | Questions, polls, debates |
| **TravelFrame/Brand** | 15% | Conversion bridge | BTS, results, subtle CTA |

### Posting Format Matrix

| Type | Format | Khi nào | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hot Take** | Text only, 1-3 câu | 2-3x/tuần | "Hanoi doesn't need 5 days. But it deserves more than 1." |
| **Micro-Guide** | Text + 1 ảnh | 3-4x/tuần | "3 things to do before 8 AM in Hanoi:" + ảnh Hoan Kiem |
| **Story/Anecdote** | Text 4-6 câu | 2x/tuần | "A guest told me she was scared of crossing the road..." |
| **Question** | Text, 1 câu | 2-3x/tuần | "First-timers in Hanoi: what surprised you most?" |
| **Cross-share IG** | Text + mention IG post | Khi có IG Reel mới | "Just posted a guide to Train Street on IG → go check" |
| **Brand bridge** | Text + 1 ảnh portfolio | 1x/tuần | Before/after, tour moment |

## 5. Reply Strategy — "15 Minutes Rule"

### Mỗi ngày dành 15-20 phút reply trên Threads:

| Target | Tìm ở đâu | Reply style |
|---|---|---|
| **Travel creators lớn** | Tìm posts về Vietnam/SEA | Thêm local perspective thật |
| **Solo travel accounts** | Tag #SoloTravel, #SoloFemaleTravel | Share Hanoi tips cụ thể |
| **"Planning Vietnam" posts** | Search "vietnam trip", "hanoi" | Helpful answer + "I live here" |
| **Photo/creator accounts** | Tag #TravelPhotography | Share photo spots/tips |
| **Food accounts** | Tag #StreetFood, #FoodTravel | Recommend Hanoi dishes |

### Reply Formula

```
[Validate their point] + [Add one unique local insight] + [No CTA]
```

**Ví dụ:**
> Người khác post: "Vietnam is on my bucket list for 2027"
> 
> Reply: "It should be. If you're doing Hanoi, give it at least 3 days — 
> the city takes a day to click, and then you won't want to leave.
> The best part isn't the landmarks. It's the mornings."

**KHÔNG làm:**
- Reply "Follow us for tips!"
- Reply "Book a tour with us!"
- Spam CTA trong replies

## 7. Cross-Pollination: Threads ↔ Instagram

### Threads → Instagram
- Share best-performing Threads posts to IG Stories (screenshot + "more on Threads")
- Khi Threads post có nhiều replies → tạo IG Reel từ topic đó
- Threads conversations = content research cho IG Reels

### Instagram → Threads
- Mỗi khi post IG Reel mới → tạo Threads companion post
  - Không share link (kills engagement)
  - Viết text version của tip + "Full video on our IG"
- Cross-share IG carousel highlights as text summaries

### Ví dụ Cross-Pollination

| IG Content | Threads Companion |
|---|---|
| Reel: "5 Tourist Scams in Hanoi" | "The #1 scam tourists fall for in Hanoi? The shoe repair trick. Here's how to handle it calmly..." |
| Carousel: "24h Hanoi" | "If you only have 24 hours in Hanoi, start at 5:30 AM. The lake is a different place before the city wakes up..." |
| Reel: "Train Street Guide" | "Quick note about Hanoi Train Street: it's a real neighborhood, not a tourist attraction. Here's what that means..." |
