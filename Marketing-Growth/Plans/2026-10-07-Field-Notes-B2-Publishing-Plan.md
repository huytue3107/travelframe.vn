### Part 07 — Taxi: Driver Passing Your Stop · `taxi-pass-destination_v02.mp4`
Thumbnail: `taxi-pass-destination_v02_thumb.png` ("TAXI IN VIETNAM / Driver passing by?")

**Caption**
```
Your driver is about to pass your stop. You have 3 seconds. What do you say? 🚕💨

⚡ Fast: "Cho mình xuống đây!" = Let me off here!
🙏 Polite: "Cho mình xuống ở đây nhé." = Please let me off here.
💛 Then: "Cảm ơn anh / chị" = Thank you

That's all you need. No panic, no awkward pointing.

📌 Save this before your next Grab or taxi ride.
💬 Comment "HANOI" and we'll send you our local Hanoi guide.

#VietnamTravel #LearnVietnamese #HanoiTravel #GrabVietnam #TravelFrameVietnam
```
