# Backlink Outreach Kit

## Trước khi gửi

1. **Domain.** Link trong thư đang trỏ tới `travelframe.sidevn.com` vì `travelframe.vn` chưa có DNS (kiểm tra 09/11). Có hai lựa chọn:
   - (a) Gửi ngay. Khi chuyển domain, 301 vẫn chuyển phần lớn giá trị link.
   - (b) Chờ chuyển domain xong rồi đổi URL trong thư. Đề xuất: gửi thư 1 (Train Street) ngay vì tin còn mới; các thư khác chờ domain.
2. **Người gửi.** Gửi từ email thật của người ký (ví dụ `admin@side-agency.com`, đang ghi trên trang Contact), thay `[Your name]`.
3. **Mỗi thư gửi riêng**, đọc bài của người nhận trước, sửa câu mở đầu cho đúng sự thật. Không gửi hàng loạt, không trả tiền đổi link, không đổi link qua lại (link exchange).
4. **Ghi kết quả** vào [tracker](2026-11-09-Backlink-Outreach-Tracker.csv) ngay sau khi gửi. Follow-up một lần sau 7 ngày nếu chưa có hồi âm, rồi dừng.

## D. Bài guest post (nháp, dành cho vietadvisor.travel, chỉ gửi khi được nhận)

**Golden Hour in Hanoi: Where to Be at Sunrise and Sunset for Better Travel Photos**

Hanoi looks its best in the first and last hour of daylight. The heat softens, the light turns warm, and the streets feel calmer. Here are six places worth planning around, and what to expect at each.

**1. Hoàn Kiếm Lake at sunrise.** Locals arrive before the sun for exercise, tai chi and badminton. Shoot along the shore toward the Huc Bridge and Ngọc Sơn Temple while the water is still. Be respectful: photograph people from a distance, or ask first.

**2. Long Biên Bridge at sunrise.** The old steel bridge catches early light from the east. Walk the pedestrian edge, stay away from the rail track, and watch for motorbikes on the narrow lanes.

**3. The Opera House square in the morning.** Before traffic builds, the yellow façade glows in soft light. A wide lens or the phone's 0.5x setting fits the whole building from across the square.

**4. West Lake at sunset.** The western sky over the lake gives the best sunsets in the city. The path near Trấn Quốc Pagoda is popular, so arrive 30 minutes early for a calm spot.

**5. Phùng Hưng railway arches in late afternoon.** The old brick arches along Phùng Hưng street hold painted murals. Late-afternoon side light brings out the texture. This is a public street, not the railway itself.

**6. Red River flower fields in season.** Along the Red River dyke, flower gardens bloom in the cooler months. Light is softest in the hour before sunset. Gardens may charge a small entry fee, so check on the day.

**Phone tips**

- Tap to expose for the brightest part of the scene, then lower the exposure slightly to keep the colors.
- Shoot into the light for silhouettes, and keep the sun behind your subject for soft portraits.
- Clean your lens. Humidity and scooters leave a film fast.

**Plan around the weather.** Hanoi's skies are often hazy, especially in winter and spring. A clear morning is worth an early alarm.
