## 2. Nội Dung Cẩm Nang Chi Tiết

### Dẫn Nhập (Current 2026 Operating Reality)
Hanoi Train Street—where century-old railway tracks run mere inches from residential doorsteps—is one of the most famous sights in Vietnam. However, it is also subject to strict safety regulations and sporadic barricades managed by local authorities to prevent accidents.

> **Important Safety Notice (Rule V-07 & V-09):**  
> We do not guarantee unconditional entry to every section of the railway. Barricades exist for human safety. However, licensed residential cafes operate legally by guiding registered guests safely inside and enforcing strict railway clearance rules. 

Here is everything you need to know about visiting, photographing, and enjoying Train Street respectfully in 2026.

---

### 1. Phân Biệt 2 Khu Vực Đường Tàu Chính Tại Hà Nội

Travelers often don't realize that "Train Street" is split into two completely separate sections:

1. **The Old Quarter Section (Tran Phu & Phung Hung Barrier):**
   - *Status:* Highly regulated. Police barriers are located at the Tran Phu intersection.
   - *How Access Works:* You cannot simply wander past the barricades alone. Local cafe owners will greet you at the boundary and escort you into their licensed establishment. You must remain seated inside the cafe boundary.
2. **The South Station Section (Le Duan & Kham Thien):**
   - *Status:* Quieter, wider, more residential. Fewer tourist cafes, but very authentic glimpse of local families cooking and washing vegetables along the tracks.
   - *Safety:* Trains travel slightly faster here; extreme situational awareness is required.

### 4. Bí Kíp Chụp Ảnh Đẹp Mà Không Cần Đứng Trên Ray Khi Tàu Đến

- **Góc Máy Từ Ban Công Tầng 2:** Nhiều quán cà phê có ban công nhỏ tầng 2 (như các quán gần số 5 Trần Phú). Từ đây, bạn có thể chụp ảnh nhìn xuống toàn cảnh đoàn tàu uốn lượn qua hẻm núi nhà cao tầng mà tuyệt đối an toàn.
- **Ảnh Chân Dung Trước Giờ Tàu (The Pre-Train Portrait):** Hãy chụp những bức ảnh dạo bước, ngồi nhâm nhi cà phê trước khi tàu đến khoảng 45 phút khi không gian yên ắng và ánh sáng còn chan hòa.
