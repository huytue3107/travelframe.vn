### Section 1: The TravelFrame Couple Aesthetic: Romantic, Unfiltered, Timeless
Many couples tell us they dread couple shoots because traditional photographers make them hold stiff poses for minutes on end until their smiles freeze. 

At TravelFrame, our approach is inspired by European cinema and documentary street photography:
- **Movement-Based Prompting:** We ask you to whisper a funny memory in your partner's ear, walk hand-in-hand across an ancient stone bridge, or share a bowl of sweet lotus seed dessert.
- **Natural Connection:** The magic happens in the moments *between* the poses—when you look at each other and laugh spontaneously.
- **Atmospheric Scenery:** We blend your relationship into the living texture of Hanoi: soft sunlight filtering through towering French mahogany trees, vintage scooters idling, and old wooden balconies draped in bougainvillea.

- **Vibe:** Traditional, cultural, timeless.
