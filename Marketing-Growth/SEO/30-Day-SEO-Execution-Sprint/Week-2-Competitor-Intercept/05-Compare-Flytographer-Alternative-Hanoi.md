However, when planning a trip to Southeast Asia—and specifically to **Hanoi, Vietnam**—travelers quickly discover two major bottlenecks with global platforms:

2. **Limited Local Availability:** In Hanoi, global platforms frequently list "temporarily unavailable" statuses or rely on a tiny roster of freelance contractors who may not be native to Hanoi's historic quarters.

Here is an honest, side-by-side comparison of booking through a global middleman platform versus choosing **TravelFrame**, a boutique Hanoi-based visual collective.
