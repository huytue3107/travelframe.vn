Small fixes, in order

Shorten title tags exceeding 60 characters medium

Google search results truncate titles longer than 60 characters. 39 pages currently exceed this limit. For example, the homepage title is 70 characters: Private Hanoi Photo Tours & Vacation Photography | TravelFrame Vietnam, which cuts off before the brand name.

Fix: Shorten the homepage title to Hanoi Photo Tours & Vacation Photography | TravelFrame (54 characters) in Drupal Metatag settings. Front-load the core service and brand.

Fix heading level skips across blog guides medium

Heading tags structure content for search crawlers and screen readers. 31 articles skip heading levels, jumping directly from <h2> to <h4> (skipping <h3>). For example, on /news/bun-cha-chan-local-culinary-guide, section <h2>Introduction...</h2> is followed directly by <h4>1. The Historical and Cultural Context</h4>.

Fix: In the Drupal rich text editor, change major section headings in these guides from Heading 4 to Heading 3 to maintain a strict H1 → H2 → H3 hierarchy.

Add descriptive alt text to inline guide images low

Alt text (image descriptions) helps search engines understand image context and enables screen readers for visually impaired travelers. 4 articles have inline images completely missing the alt attribute, such as <img src="/sites/default/files/inline-images/2_0.jpg"> in the Bun Cha culinary guide.

Fix: Edit the 4 affected articles (Imperial Citadel, Cam Phu Cau, Bun Cha, and Santorini Park), click the image, and add a descriptive 5–10 word caption in the Alternative Text field (e.g., "Traditional bowl of Hanoi bun cha with grilled pork and herbs").

Expand thin content on utility pages low

Pages with very few words can be viewed by search crawlers as low-value or placeholder pages. /about has only 123 words and /guides has only 140 words.

Fix: Expand /about to 350+ words highlighting TravelFrame's story, local photographer background, candid documentary philosophy, and transparent pricing. Either expand /guides with category descriptions and featured reading lists or add a noindex tag until populated.

Trim meta descriptions over 160 characters low

Search engines truncate snippets past ~160 characters. 5 pages exceed this threshold, including /about (243 characters) and /news/hanoi-old-quarter-guide (168 characters).

Fix: Shorten the Old Quarter description to: "A local guide to Hanoi's Old Quarter: street navigation, night market tips, uncrowded photo spots, and authentic food recommendations." (137 characters).
