# Marketing Growth

## Purpose

Quản lý campaign, content, distribution và growth loop.

## Responsibilities

Calendar, brief, draft, review, approved content, CTA và published ledger.

## Inputs

Approved strategy, research insights, production deliverables và performance data.

## Outputs

Campaign package, publish-ready content và distribution records.

## Source of truth

Campaign manifest và `Published-Ledger.md`; render đơn lẻ không phải publish record.

## Folder structure

`Campaigns/`, `Content/Drafts`, `Content/Review`, `Content/Approved`,
`Content/Carousels`, `Plans/`.

## Workflow

Brief -> draft -> claim check -> review -> approval -> production -> publish -> learn.

## Approval requirements

Rocky duyệt publish và claim thương mại chưa có approved source.

## Naming conventions

Campaign dùng `CMP-YYYYMM-SHORT-SLUG`; content dùng dated slug.

## Archive policy

Giữ campaign results và source pointers khi archive.

## Related departments

Brand-Strategy, Research-Insights, Creative-Production, Sales-CRM, Data-Analytics.
