## Measurable design

- 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, 9:16.
- Voice duration determines the timeline; keep approximately 1.3 seconds of visual/music tail.
- First frame: question already visible. No logo-only opening.
- Headlines 65 px, captions 58 px; captions at y=1190 within the phone-safe reading band. Use 3–6 spoken words per group and a red active-word highlight.
- Two white photo frames per scene; photos never generated substitutes for named dishes or real landmarks. Vary left/right anchoring and crop.
- Photo entrances 0.4–0.45 seconds, cat pop 0.4 seconds, photo punch 1.12× on selected beats, camera flash no more than 30% white for 0.1 seconds in selected scenes.
- Original synthesized music and pop/whoosh/shutter cues. Voice gain 1, bed 0.12, effects 0.07–0.10 as a starting mix; measure the final output.
- No breathing loops, moving landed headlines, generic full-screen gradient cards, full-width subtitle bars, falsely labelled stock clients, or unsafe railway walking imagery.
