# Vietnam weather: October–December

Status: Review. Kịch bản do Rocky cung cấp; giữ nội dung, chỉnh câu về áo ấm theo nguồn bên dưới. Chưa duyệt đăng.

Planning a Vietnam trip between October and December?

Here’s something to know before you pack: the weather can feel very different from north to south.

In Northern Vietnam, Hanoi usually starts to feel cooler in autumn. It’s a lovely time to wander the Old Quarter, stop for coffee and explore with your camera. By December, pack layers and a jacket — and warmer clothing if you’re heading to the mountains.

In Central Vietnam, places like Huế, Đà Nẵng and Hội An can see heavy rain and storms during the later months of the year. Keep your plans flexible, and have a café or indoor activity in mind. Rain can continue into December, so check the forecast before you go.

In Southern Vietnam, it stays warm. You may still see showers in October and November, while December generally brings drier weather.

Wherever you’re going, check the local forecast close to your trip.

And if Hanoi is on your list, save a little time to slow down, explore its streets and capture the moments in between.
