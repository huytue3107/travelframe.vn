# Vietnam weather · v02 visual revision

Date: 2026-11-05. Status: Review. User request: more framed illustration, stronger captions and more visual effects.

The edit uses 36 photo placements across 18 scenes, drawn from 14 distinct photographs. Eight new Pexels images cover Hội An, Sa Pa, Ho Chi Minh City, packing and umbrella detail. Their source pages, creators and intended context are in `assets/new-photo-ledger-v02.json`. Three additional existing library images show Long Bien Bridge, St Joseph's Cathedral and a Ho Chi Minh City street photographer. The latter is used in the southern section, not represented as Hanoi or as a TravelFrame photographer. Terraced fields illustrate the northern highlands; they do not promise harvest colours in December. The umbrella is a generic rain detail, not a report of flooding or a weather event in a named city.

Caption typography changes from long white text on black bars to 49 shorter groups with large black lettering, white stroke and red word highlighting timed to ElevenLabs character alignment. The narration and factual script stay as v01; there is no new paid voice call. Headings are rewritten as short hooks without adding a forecast guarantee.

Three collage arrangements alternate. Every scene has two separately animated photos and a cat sticker. Effects include photo slide-ins, stack arrivals, limited punch-ins inside photo windows, tilt changes, underline reveals and brief camera-style light accents in six scenes. Original synthesized shutter cues join the existing original pop and whoosh sounds. No outside music recording is added.

The v01 MP4, original `build.mjs` and original source scene files remain. `index-v01.html.txt` preserves the v01 root source. `build-v02.mjs` generates the current root and separate `compositions/v02/` files. Do not run the v01 builder over v02 without explicitly choosing that restoration.

Original source sidecars for the additional library photos: Pexels 26919111 (tu nguyen), 31833270 (Nguyen Duc Toan), 36418084 (Tuan Vy), retained in `Creative-Production/Assets/Stock/`. Review source permissions and identifiable people before publication.
