# 1. Brief gửi khách mời (tiếng Anh)

**Hi! Thanks for joining TravelFrame for a food shoot in Hanoi.**

What we are making: four short Instagram and Facebook videos about where to eat international food in Hanoi (omakase, Indian, halal, pizza and Korean BBQ).

Your role: you play a traveler. No lines to learn. We will ask you to eat, react, order and walk. Our narrator records the voice later.

Schedule:
- **Day 1:** about 08:00–15:30 (West Lake brunch, a pho shop, Indian restaurants in the Old Quarter).
- **Day 2:** about 15:00–21:30 (an omakase counter, the Old Quarter, Hanoi's Japanese and Korean neighborhoods).
- You do not need to stay for every location. We will mark the scenes you are in.

What to wear:
- Casual, plain colors (no big logos or slogans). Bring one extra top for day 2.
- For the omakase scene: smart casual, no strong perfume.
- For the Old Quarter halal scenes: shoulders and knees covered.

Food: please tell us about allergies or diet rules before the shoot. We cover the food you eat on camera.

What you get: a personal photo set from the shoot, edited and delivered within 48 hours.

**Your rights:** you can say no to any scene, and you can stop at any time. If you change your mind later, tell us and we will stop using new posts with you.
