# Production specification

- Goal: invite international visitors to enjoy small Hanoi moments and consider a local photo walk.
- Output: portrait 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 35.7 seconds; scenes aligned to ElevenLabs character-level timings.
- Visual: Pexels video and photographs already in TravelFrame's library; teal #008AA3 and white Inter over a legible charcoal text backing. Text stays above the platform caption zone.
- Motion: moving Hanoi street, food and lakeside footage alternates with two photo shots; text enters over 0.35 seconds. First frame includes the hook.
- Story: playful reverse advice; coffee → Old Quarter → pho → Hoan Kiem Lake → local photographer, ending in one DM CTA.
- Audio: ElevenLabs Adam, English, multilingual v2. A single 553-character generation was used. No music bed: no reusable music with confirmed rights was identified.
- Banned: invented prices and guarantees; customer photos without permission; generated images presented as real places; copying the source transcript; publishing.
- Review: check start, each cut, end, safe area, spelling, asset provenance, narration sync and audio levels.
