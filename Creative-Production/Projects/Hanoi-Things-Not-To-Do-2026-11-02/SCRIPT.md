# Five things you shouldn't do in Hanoi

Status: Draft. English voiceover for ElevenLabs Adam. Inspiration: [KissTour Vietnam, 5 Things NOT to Do in Vietnam](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/XzwrKbzVvL4). The wording, places and TravelFrame offer are an original adaptation. Source subtitles are automatic and imperfect.

Five things you shouldn't do in Hanoi.

First, don't order Vietnamese coffee. One cup can easily become a daily ritual.

Second, don't wander the Old Quarter. You might lose track of time in its little streets.

Third, don't stop for pho. Before you finish, you'll already be thinking about your next bowl.

Fourth, don't take a walk around Hoan Kiem Lake. Your camera roll may never recover.

And finally, don't explore Hanoi with a local photographer, unless you want to take home a story that feels like yours. Message TravelFrame to plan your photo walk.

Delivery: playful, warm, clear, not alarmist. The narration is a creative invitation, not a price, safety, or guaranteed outcome claim.
