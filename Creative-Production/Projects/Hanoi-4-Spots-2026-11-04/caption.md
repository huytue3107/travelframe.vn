4 iconic Hanoi spots you can easily explore in one day — with the local tips most guides skip. 🚶🏻‍♀️🇻🇳

1. Long Biên Bridge 🌉
Over 120 years old, built with vintage steel arches and railway tracks. Sunset light here turns everything golden and industrial — pure cinematic magic.

2. Hanoi Train Street 🚂
Trains pass inches from doorsteps! Enter from the quieter far end for a relaxed coffee, or head deeper inside for the vibrant lantern glow at night.

3. St. Joseph’s Cathedral ⛪
Hanoi’s 1886 neo-gothic icon. Free entry through the side door (closed midday), and stunning illuminated vaulted arches once dusk settles. Grab a warm bánh mì right nearby!

4. Hoàn Kiếm Lake & Old Quarter 🌳
Stroll past the French colonial heritage facades, Turtle Tower, and ancient banyan trees. By day or night, getting lost in these little alleys is where the real charm begins.
