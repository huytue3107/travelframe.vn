Only one day in Hanoi?
Here is the move.

Start early at Dong Xuan Market.
Grab a local breakfast, browse the stalls, and get lost in the Old Quarter.

By late morning, walk down to Hoan Kiem Lake.
Take the scenic loop, then stop for egg coffee or a cold Vietnamese coffee.

In the afternoon, slow things down at the Temple of Literature.
It is calm, beautiful, and a nice break from the scooter traffic.

At sunset, swing by St. Joseph's Cathedral and the French Quarter.
This is your polished, postcard-worthy Hanoi moment.

End the day with Train Street or a late-night street-food crawl.
Check local access before you go, respect the neighborhood, and keep your plans flexible.
