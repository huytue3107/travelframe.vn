First time in Hanoi?
Skip the tourist-trap checklist and start here.

Number three: Dong Xuan Market.
It is big, busy, and has zero chill in the best way.
Browse local snacks, fabrics, souvenirs, and street eats.
Pro move: go early, carry small cash, and ask before taking photos.

Number two: Hoan Kiem Lake.
It is the perfect reset after the market chaos.
Take a slow walk, grab an egg coffee nearby, and watch Hanoi wake up.

Number one: Train Street.
It is wild, iconic, and definitely worth seeing.
But this is a real neighborhood, not a theme park.
Follow the barriers, listen to local security, and never step on the tracks.
