# TravelFrame Hanoi Reels Design

## Format

- Canvas: 1080x1920
- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Safe area: keep primary text between x=84 and x=996, y=170 and y=1640

## Visual identity

- Background charcoal teal: `#10272B`
- Main text off-white: `#F4F1EA`
- TravelFrame accent teal: `#008AA3`
- Small warm highlight: `#F2B66D`
- Use flat color blocks and readable contrast. No gradients.
- Type: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif
- Headlines: bold, compact, 74px to 96px
- Supporting copy: 42px to 54px
- Micro labels: 28px to 34px, uppercase, increased tracking

## Motion

- Use 0.3s to 0.6s entrances with varied easing.
- Every scene gets an entrance animation.
- Use a teal wipe between scenes and a clean final hold.
- Keep captions short enough to scan on a phone.

## Content guardrails

- Voice: warm local friend, practical, lightly playful.
- Do not promise Train Street access or fixed train times.
- Describe market footage as market B-roll unless the visual source clearly proves Dong Xuan Market.
- Do not add unverified prices, schedules, or claims.
- End with a save/share action and the TravelFrame name.
