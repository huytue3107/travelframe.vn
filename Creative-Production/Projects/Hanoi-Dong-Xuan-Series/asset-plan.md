# Hanoi Dong Xuan Video Series Asset Plan

## Shared direction

- Format: 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 30 fps.
- Voice: English narration with VBee voice code `n_hanoi_male_huytue20260719134424408_advertise_vc`.
- Tone: warm local friend, short sentences, practical, lightly playful.
- Brand bridge: end with `TravelFrame — your local friend in Hanoi`.
- Safety: do not promise Train Street access or fixed train times; show barriers and respectful behavior.
- Text: keep on-screen copy short and readable; use natural wrapping inside a safe-area container.

## Video 1: Top 3 Hanoi Spots

1. Opening: Hanoi street or Old Quarter movement.
2. Dong Xuan Market: market exterior, stalls, hands browsing, street food.
3. Hoan Kiem Lake: walking loop, lake, local morning movement, coffee.
4. Train Street: rail-side neighborhood detail from a safe distance, barriers, passing train only if licensed footage clearly supports it.
5. Closing: Hanoi night texture or walking shot with save/share CTA.

## Video 2: One-Day Hanoi Itinerary

1. Opening: Hanoi morning arrival or Old Quarter walk.
2. Morning: Dong Xuan Market and Old Quarter lanes.
3. Late morning: Hoan Kiem Lake and coffee.
4. Afternoon: Temple of Literature.
5. Sunset: Hanoi Opera House or French Quarter.
6. Night: Train Street or street-food crawl, with flexible-access note.

## Footage rules

- Prefer Pexels videos returned by the authenticated Video API.
- Select portrait clips first; use landscape clips only when the subject remains crop-safe.
- Keep a `pexels-results.json` record with each clip URL, video ID, creator, and chosen file URL.
- Do not label generic Vietnam footage as Dong Xuan Market, Hoan Kiem Lake, Temple of Literature, or Train Street unless the visual evidence is clear.
