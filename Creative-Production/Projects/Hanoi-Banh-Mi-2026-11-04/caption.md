Craving the perfect crispy Bánh Mì in Hanoi? 🥖🇻🇳

With warm, crackling baguettes, rich savory pâté, and fresh herbs, Bánh Mì is the ultimate Vietnamese street food ritual. If you’re visiting Hanoi, here are 3 local spots food lovers can't miss:

1️⃣ Bánh Mì Lan (14E Hàng Chuối)
An authentic local hidden gem with incredible flavor at local prices (~20,000 VND / $0.76). Simple, traditional, and packed with old-school charm.

2️⃣ Bánh Mì 25 (25 Hàng Cá)
Famous for generous fillings, fresh ingredients, and a relaxed Old Quarter cafe atmosphere (~35,000 VND / $1.33). Perfect for first-timers!

3️⃣ Bánh Mì Phố (64 Hàng Bông)
Warm street corner energy loved by both locals and travelers (~35,000 VND / $1.33). Great crust and rich, satisfying sauces.
