HANOI OLD QUARTER: A 2-HOUR PHOTO WALK

4 zones · 6 stops · 1.9 km on foot (29 min walking) · start 7:00 for soft light and quiet streets

  • 1 Hàng Chiếu & Đồng Xuân
  • 2 Mã Mây
  • 3 Hàng Bạc
  • 4 Tạ Hiện
  1. S
    7:00

    Hoàn Kiếm Lake, north end

    Start where Hàng Đào leaves the lake, at the Cầu Gỗ corner. From first light, locals jog, dance and practise tai chi around the lake.

  2. 1
    7:20 Zone 1

    Đồng Xuân Market

    Walk north on Hàng Đào, Hàng Ngang and Hàng Đường, the night-market route on Friday–Sunday evenings. Đồng Xuân is the city's largest covered market; the food stalls around its edges are the draw.

  3. 2
    7:40 Zone 1

    Ô Quan Chưởng gate

    Follow Hàng Chiếu east. First built in 1749, it is the only old city gate still standing, and motorbikes still pass through it.

  4. 3
    7:55 Zone 2

    87 Mã Mây heritage house

    A restored merchant tube house: narrow at the front, deep at the back, with small courtyards. Entry about 20,000 VND. Check the fee and opening hours at the door.

  5. 4
    8:15 Zone 3

    Hàng Bạc and Đình Kim Ngân

    Hàng Bạc was the silver street. Đình Kim Ngân, the communal house at 42 Hàng Bạc, honours the founders of the silversmith trade.

  6. 5
    8:30 Zone 4

    Tạ Hiện

    Quiet in the morning. After dark it fills with low plastic stools, cold beer and music, so come back in the evening.

  7. 6
    8:45

    Bạch Mã Temple, 76 Hàng Buồm

    One of the oldest temples in the quarter. Free to enter; usually closed at lunch. Dress modestly.

Finish around 9:00. Back to the lake: about 7 minutes on foot (466 m) south via Hàng Ngang and Hàng Đào. Times are approximate.

PHOTO TIPS

  • Look for yellow facades, green shutters and lanterns; early light is soft and the streets are emptier.
  • Check the top of your frame: stray power cables can cut across a portrait.
  • Smile and say "Xin chào" before photographing people or shops. If they say no, move on.
